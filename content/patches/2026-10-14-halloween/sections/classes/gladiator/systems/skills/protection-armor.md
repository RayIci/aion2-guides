**Passive skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-protection-armor-2026-10].

Increases the caster's Block by 200 and restores an amount of HP on Block. Cooldown: 1s (the data page does not show the HP amount for this level) [@gamingtools-skill-protection-armor-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Max skill level | 40 |
