**Active skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 174 + 163.6% Attack damage to a target afflicted with Knockdown within 4m and increases Enmity. Has a 7% chance to trigger when attacking a target with Incapacitated Immunity. 5 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 5s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10].

- Absorbs 0.7% HP (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10]
- Changes to AoE Skill (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10]
- Adds **Upward Strike** Chain Skill (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10]
- Critical Hit on hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10]
- Remove cooldown (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10]
