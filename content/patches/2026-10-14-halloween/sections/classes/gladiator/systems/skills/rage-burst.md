**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-rage-burst-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 1327 + 218% Attack damage to a target within 4m. Inflicts Wounded for 10s on hit, reducing the enemy's Attack by 10%, and increases the caster's PvE Damage Boost by 10% and PvP Damage Boost by 5% for 10s. Triggers **Overhead Slam** on the target for 10s on hit. Wounded does not stack with the Shrink effect from the Templar skill **Taunt**. 20 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-rage-burst-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 45s |
| Cost | 100 MP |
| Max skill level | 25 |

### Specializations

- -15s **Rage Burst** cooldown (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-rage-burst-2026-10]
- +10% Attack reduction (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-rage-burst-2026-10]
- Critical Hit on hit (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-rage-burst-2026-10]
- +10% PvE Damage Boost increase and +5% PvP Damage Boost increase (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-rage-burst-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-rage-burst-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-rage-burst-2026-10].
