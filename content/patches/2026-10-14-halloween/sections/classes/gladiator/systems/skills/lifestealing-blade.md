**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-lifestealing-blade-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 1522 + 250% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 4m and absorbs 10% HP. 20 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-lifestealing-blade-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 10 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cooldown | 90s |
| Cost | 200 MP |
| Max skill level | 25 |

### Specializations

- Up to +20% damage when more targets hit (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-lifestealing-blade-2026-10]
- Predation effect on party members for 20s (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-lifestealing-blade-2026-10]
- -30s cooldown (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-lifestealing-blade-2026-10]
- +5% HP absorbed (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-lifestealing-blade-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-lifestealing-blade-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-lifestealing-blade-2026-10].
