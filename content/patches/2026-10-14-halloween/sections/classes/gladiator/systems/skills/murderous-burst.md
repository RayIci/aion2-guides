**Passive skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-murderous-burst-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Grants a stack of Menace for 3s when landing an attack. Removes Menace at 5 stacks and deals 539 + 78% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 5m of the caster and increases the caster's Critical Damage Boost by 5.5% for 3s. [@gamingtools-skill-murderous-burst-2026-10]
