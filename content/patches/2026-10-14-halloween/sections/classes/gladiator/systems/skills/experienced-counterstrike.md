**Passive skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-experienced-counterstrike-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Increases Front Attack Damage Boost by 0.4%. Increases PvE Damage Boost by 5.4%, PvP Damage Boost by 2.7% for 20s for the caster and party members on Block. PvE Damage Boost and PvP Damage Boost does not stack with Templar's **Fury** effect. [@gamingtools-skill-experienced-counterstrike-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Max skill level | 40 |
