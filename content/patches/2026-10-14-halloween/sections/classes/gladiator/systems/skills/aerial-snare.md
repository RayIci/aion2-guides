**Active skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-aerial-snare-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 479 + 181.5% Attack damage to a target afflicted with Knockdown within 4m with a 50% chance to inflict Airborne for 3s. 100% chance to inflict Airborne on NPC targets. Has a 5% chance to trigger on attacking a target with Incapacitated Immunity. 20 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-aerial-snare-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 45s |
| Cost | 150 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-aerial-snare-2026-10].

- Multi-Hit on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-aerial-snare-2026-10]
- +25% **Aerial Snare** Airborne Chance (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-aerial-snare-2026-10]
- +100% damage to targets with Incapacitated Immunity (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-aerial-snare-2026-10]
- Adds **Forced Fall** Chain Skill (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-aerial-snare-2026-10]
- -50% **Aerial Snare** cooldown (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-aerial-snare-2026-10]
