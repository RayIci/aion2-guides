**Passive skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-survival-stance-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Increases the caster's HP by 200. Increases Max HP by an additional 7%, Natural HP Regen by 20, PvE Damage Tolerance by 5.5%, and PvP Damage Tolerance by 2.75%. [@gamingtools-skill-survival-stance-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Max skill level | 40 |
