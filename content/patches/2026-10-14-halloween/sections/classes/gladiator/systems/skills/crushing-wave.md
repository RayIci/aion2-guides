**Active skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-crushing-wave-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 206 + 105% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 5m of the caster. 5 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-crushing-wave-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Circle 5 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cooldown | 20s |
| Cost | 250 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |
| Chain role | Opening Skill |
| Chains into | Frenzied Wave |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-crushing-wave-2026-10].

- Absorbs 2% HP (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-crushing-wave-2026-10]
- Restores 50 MP on landing a Critical Hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-crushing-wave-2026-10]
- Up to +20% damage when more targets hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-crushing-wave-2026-10]
- Resets cooldown on landing a Critical Hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-crushing-wave-2026-10]
- 50% chance to pull (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-crushing-wave-2026-10]
