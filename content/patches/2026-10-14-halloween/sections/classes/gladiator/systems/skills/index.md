## How skills grow

- **Skill Points (SP):** each active and passive skill goes from level 2 to 10 for 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 4, 4 and 4 SP, so 21 SP per skill. Each step also needs a character level, three levels apart, starting at the skill's unlock level [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-murderous-burst-2026-10].
- **Max skill level:** 40 for active and passive skills and 25 for Stigma skills; the data pages list SP learning only up to level 10 and Stigma learning up to level 20 [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10].
- **Specializations:** active skills open Specialization slots at skill level 8, 12 and 20. Every active skill except Dodge has five Specializations: three available at skill level 8, one at 12 and one at 16 [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10]. Stigma skills have four Specializations at skill levels 5, 10, 15 and 20 [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10].
- **Stigma levels** cost Stigma points: 1 for levels 1–5, 2 for 6–10, 4 for 11–15 and 8 for 16–20 [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10].
