**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-wave-armor-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 207 + 34% Attack Damage over Time to up to 4 enemies within 3m of the caster for 10s. [@gamingtools-skill-wave-armor-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Cooldown | 60s |
| Cost | 200 MP |
| Max skill level | 25 |

### Specializations

- Absorbs 2% HP (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-wave-armor-2026-10]
- x1.5 **Experienced Counterstrike** effect for the duration (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-wave-armor-2026-10]
- x1.5 **Murderous Burst** effect for the duration (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-wave-armor-2026-10]
- Reflects 10% of damage taken (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-wave-armor-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-wave-armor-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-wave-armor-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build; PvP picks are on each PvP mode's build page.
