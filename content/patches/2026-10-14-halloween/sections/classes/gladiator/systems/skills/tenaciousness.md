**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-tenaciousness-2026-10].

At skill level 1: The caster becomes immune to damage and status effects for 3s. Increases PvE Damage Tolerance by 50% and PvP Damage Tolerance by 25% for 3s when the effect ends. Cannot block certain powerful attacks. Unaffected by cooldown increase effects. Tenaciousness, Salvation, and Barrier Spell effects cannot be applied for 30s if Invincibility stacking is blocked. [@gamingtools-skill-tenaciousness-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Cooldown | 150s |
| Max skill level | 25 |

### Specializations

- +100% Incoming Heal (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-tenaciousness-2026-10]
- Restores 20% Max HP when the effect ends (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-tenaciousness-2026-10]
- +20% Attack (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-tenaciousness-2026-10]
- Trigger **Tenaciousness** when afflicted with Impact-type status (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-tenaciousness-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-tenaciousness-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-tenaciousness-2026-10].
