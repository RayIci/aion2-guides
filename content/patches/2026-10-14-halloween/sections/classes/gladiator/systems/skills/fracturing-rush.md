**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-fracturing-rush-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Rushes forward 10m and deals 974 + 160% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies on the path, with a 60% chance to inflict Root for 3s. 100% chance to inflict Root on NPC targets. 20 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-fracturing-rush-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 12 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cooldown | 90s |
| Cost | 100 MP |
| Max skill level | 25 |

### Specializations

- +2 max targets hit (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-fracturing-rush-2026-10]
- Adds **Destructive Rush** Chain Skill (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-fracturing-rush-2026-10]
- +20% Root Chance (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-fracturing-rush-2026-10]
- 50% chance to inflict Airborne for 3s (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-fracturing-rush-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-fracturing-rush-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-fracturing-rush-2026-10].
