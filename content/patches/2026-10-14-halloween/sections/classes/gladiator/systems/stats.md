The Gladiator's base stats grow every level. These are the class's own numbers before gear, Daevanion and other bonuses [@gamingtools-gladiator-2026-10].

| Character level | Attack Bonus | Defense Bonus | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | 10 | 100 |
| 5 | 11 | 50 | 502 |
| 10 | 17 | 100 | 1,010 |
| 22 | 32 | 220 | 2,248 |
| 32 | 45 | 320 | 3,302 |
| 37 | 51 | 370 | 3,836 |
| 45 | 61 | 450 | 4,702 |

From level 1 to 45 that is +55 Attack Bonus, +440 Defense Bonus and +4,602 HP [@gamingtools-gladiator-2026-10].

What each stat does in combat, and the soft and hard caps, are not written yet [!unverified].
