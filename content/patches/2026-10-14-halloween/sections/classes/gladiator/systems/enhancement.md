Enhancement raises a piece of gear by consuming Enhance Stones (from level 9). The maximum enhancement level depends on the gear's grade, and each level costs more stones than the last [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

What to enhance first and how far, by stage, is not written yet [!unverified].
