Enhancement raises a piece of gear by consuming Enhance Stones (from level 9). The maximum level depends on the gear's grade, and each level costs more stones than the last [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10]. Each level adds 1 item level [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].

## Success chances

For an item-level-86 piece (Abyssal Boots) [@gamingtools-enchant-simulator-2026-10]:

| Step | Chance |
|---|---|
| +0 → +10 | 100% each |
| +10 → +11 | 65% |
| +11 → +12 | 50% |
| +12 → +13 | 35% |
| +13 → +14 | 25% |
| +14 → +15 | 20% |

From +0 to +15 that is about 22 attempts, 12.6 million Kina and 160,752 Enhance Stones on average; nine in ten runs finish within 27 attempts [@gamingtools-enchant-simulator-2026-10]. The simulator also has separate Exceed and Surpass tracks [@gamingtools-enchant-simulator-2026-10]. Amplify adds 5 item level per level [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].

## What to enhance, by stage

::::play
- **Leveling:** +5 on weapon and guard before anything else [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].
- **Level 45 → 1,400:** weapon, guard and bracelet to +10, the free steps [@corpus-item-level-2026-10; @gamingtools-enchant-simulator-2026-10].
- **1,400 → 2,100:** each new Vakron piece to +5, the whole set to +7 once it's complete [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
- **2,100+:** Amplify your Unique pieces [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
- Don't enhance gear you'll replace at the next dungeon [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
::::

Enhance Stones come from expedition cubes (1,000 per Krao Cave Exploration cube) and the Daily Dungeon (up to 10,000) [@gamingtools-exp-krao-cave-exploration-2026-10; @gamingtools-daily-bio-research-base-2026-10].
