AION 2 macros press a list of quick slots in order; each quick slot casts the first ready skill from its bottom row up [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].

- Set the macro once in the skill preset you play with. Manual input always beats the macro [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
- Keep your generator skill on left mouse, out of the macro, and hold it together with the macro key [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
- The game says macro step order is not guaranteed, so a macro is a priority list, not a fixed rotation [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
- Turn on Skill Queue (Settings → Combat → Controls), or steps pressed during a cast are lost [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].

The macros for each build are on PvE → PvE macros and on each PvP mode's macro page.
