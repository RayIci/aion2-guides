Daevanion is a growth board that raises character stats and skill levels with Daevanion Crystals or Fragments [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10]. Completing the 2nd Ascension activates the board [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

- The Gladiator has five boards: **Nezekan**, **Zikel**, **Vaizel**, **Triniel** and **Azphel** [@gamingtools-gladiator-2026-10].
- You earn 4 Daevanion Crystals per level from level 12: 4 at level 12, 44 at 22, 84 at 32 and 136 at 45 [@gamingtools-character-levels-2026-10].

Which nodes to take first is not written yet [!unverified].
