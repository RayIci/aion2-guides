## Manastones

Each Manastone line adds item level by its colour: grey 1, green 2, blue 3, yellow 4 [@corpus-item-level-2026-10]. Before 1,400 put one grey or green line on each piece; from 1,400 aim for one blue and one green per piece [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].

## Theostones

A Rare Theostone adds 3 item level; other grades aren't confirmed [@corpus-item-level-2026-10]. Put one on weapon and guard in the 1,400–2,100 stage [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].

## Clash Runes

Two rune slots, 40 item level each. +1 always succeeds, +2 has an 80% chance, and a failure can destroy the rune [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].

## Soul Binding

The Gladiator's class data lists Soulbind skills: each Gladiator skill appears with a chance of about 4.5% to 20% and a max level of 4 [@gamingtools-gladiator-2026-10]. Creators treat it as a Combat Power step after 2,100 [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
