:::quick
1. Level in Poeta (Elyos) or Ishalgen (Asmodians) until level 10 [@fextralife-regions-2026-10].
2. Raise first: **Keen Strike** to 4, **Rending Blow** to 4, **Rush Strike** to 2, **Protection Armor** to 2, **Blood Absorption** to 2 [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].
3. Set the macro for this bracket (table under How to play), then hold left mouse and the macro key [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].
4. Tick the checklist at the bottom before you move on [!opinion].
:::

## On arrival

At level 5 the build has Keen Strike 3, Rending Blow 2, Leaping Slam 1, Mocking Blade 1, Overhead Slam 1, Sword Aura Rampage 1, Survival Stance 1, Leaping Slam 1 [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].

## How to play

::::play
- Solo leveling plan: Crushing Wave and Rending Blow clear packs; every knockdown from Mocking Blade or Wrath Wave, and every hit of Rage Burst, opens Overhead Slam, your hardest hit; Leaping Slam carries you to the next pack and Crushing Wave's HP absorb keeps you going [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].
- Hold left mouse (generator) and the macro key together; use the other skills when needed [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10]. Macro setup: [Macros](/classes/gladiator/reference/systems/macros).

## Gear

Equip quest upgrades as you get them. While leveling, aim for +5 on your weapon and guard before spending on other gear [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].
