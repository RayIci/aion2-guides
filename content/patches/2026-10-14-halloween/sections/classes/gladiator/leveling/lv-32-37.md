## On arrival

At level 32 the build has Crushing Wave 9 + 3 bonus, Ruinous Blow 6 + 1 bonus, Keen Strike 10 + 2 bonus, Rending Blow 10 + 2 bonus, Leaping Slam 8, Mocking Blade 5, Overhead Slam 8 + 2 bonus, Sword Aura Rampage 4, with Stigma Wrath Wave, Lunge Stance, Rage Burst [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].

## How to play

::::play
- Solo leveling plan: Crushing Wave and Rending Blow clear packs; every knockdown from Mocking Blade or Wrath Wave, and every hit of Rage Burst, opens Overhead Slam, your hardest hit; Leaping Slam carries you to the next pack and Crushing Wave's HP absorb keeps you going [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].
- Hold left mouse (generator) and the macro key together; use the other skills when needed [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10]. Macro setup: Systems → Macros.

## Gear

Equip quest upgrades as you get them. While leveling, aim for +5 on your weapon and guard before spending on other gear [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].

## Where to level

Verteron (Elyos) or Altgard (Asmodians), which cover levels 10–45 [@fextralife-regions-2026-10].

Dungeons that open here: Fire Temple (Exploration) at 37 [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].
