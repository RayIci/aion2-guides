:::quick{title="Quick steps"}
1. Put your skills on the keys as in the bar below [!opinion].
2. Create a macro with **key 5 only**: that row is your core combo [@corpus-gladiator-pve-build-2026-10; @corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].
3. Hold right mouse (the macro) and left mouse (Keen Strike); press everything amber yourself [@corpus-gladiator-pve-build-2026-10].
4. Skills and Stigmas: the same as every PvE setup ([Level 45: set up](/classes/gladiator/setup#pve-skills-to-raise)) [!opinion].
:::

**Who it's for:** You want the macro to do only your core damage, and to press your buffs, burst and blocks yourself. [!opinion]

Grobs's macro is one row: Rage Burst, then Overhead Slam, then Rending Blow. It tries Rage Burst first, uses Overhead Slam while it is up and falls back to Rending Blow [@corpus-gladiator-pve-build-2026-10]. He binds Leaping Slam and Rush Strike to the same key, so the second fires when the first is on cooldown [@corpus-gladiator-pve-build-2026-10]. Creators keep self-buffs, Stigma burst, dashes and charge skills out of the macro [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].

## The macro

::::play
1. Skill menu → **Macro** → **Add macro** [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].
2. Add **key 5** as the only step; delay 10 ms [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10; @gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
3. Hold it with left mouse (Keen Strike), so the game weaves in animation cancels [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].
::::

## How to play

::::play
1. Buff by hand: Lunge Stance (1), Zikel's Blessing (2) [@corpus-gladiator-pve-build-2026-10].
2. Jump in with Q, cast Ruinous Blow (3) for Prepare for Battle [@corpus-gladiator-pve-build-2026-10].
3. Block the boss's first attack with Focused Block (E) [@corpus-gladiator-pve-build-2026-10].
4. Hold right mouse (the macro) and left mouse (Keen Strike) together; once Rending Blow is at 16, Grobs holds only the macro [@corpus-gladiator-pve-build-2026-10].
5. Packs: Crushing Wave (6); a knockdown from Mocking Blade (7) opens Overhead Slam outside Rage Burst; Sword Aura Rampage (8) only on a staggered target [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-sword-aura-rampage-2026-10; !opinion].
::::
