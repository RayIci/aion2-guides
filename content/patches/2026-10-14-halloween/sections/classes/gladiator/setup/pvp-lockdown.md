**Who it's for:** Fextralife's PvP build: a frontline brawler for the Abyss and small skirmishes, built on healing reduction, knockdown chains and immunity windows [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].

## Skills and milestones

- **Overhead Slam** to 20; **Mocking Blade**, **Aerial Snare** and **Defiance** to 16 (the control core) [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].
- Stigmas: **Blade Toss** to 5/10/15 (Incoming Heal −70%, then a 5 s Slow, then breaks shields); **Armor of Balance** to 5; **Tenaciousness** to 10 (restores 20% HP when it ends); **Lunge Stance** to 10 (+20% Move Speed) [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].
- Passives: Attack Preparation, Survival Stance, Survival Willpower [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].

## Stigmas

**Lunge Stance**, **Armor of Balance**, **Tenaciousness**, **Blade Toss** [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].

Swaps:

- With a dedicated healer: swap Tenaciousness for **Rage Burst**, so Overhead Slam fires without a Knockdown [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].
- Against melee-heavy teams: swap Armor of Balance for **Focused Block** [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].

## The macro

Add only the blue keys, in order, delay 10 ms. Keep control, openers and defensive skills out of it so you can time them: creators agree on keeping defensive and mobility skills manual [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10; @fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].

## How to play

::::play
1. **Open:** Blade Toss (1) from 20 m: −30% Defense and −70% Incoming Heal for 10 s [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].
2. **Close in:** Leaping Slam or Rush Strike (Q) [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].
3. **Lock down:** Mocking Blade (2) for the Knockdown, then hold the macro: Overhead Slam only fires on a knocked-down target, so the macro waits on it [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10].
4. **Extend:** Aerial Snare (3) to launch it Airborne, then the Forced Fall chain; Ankle Slice (8) after a block for another Knockdown [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].
5. **Defend:** Armor of Balance (E) before you dive in; Tenaciousness (7) as the emergency reset; Defiance (4) only for hard control (Stun, Knockdown, Airborne, Fear) [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].
::::
