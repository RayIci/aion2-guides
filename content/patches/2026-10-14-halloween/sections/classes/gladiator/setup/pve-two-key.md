:::quick{title="Quick steps"}
1. Build the two stacked slots below: the **macro slot** (key 5) and the **buff button** (key 1) [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
2. Bind the macro key under Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro, then build the macro from the Skill screen (K) with the Macro button; add key 5 as its only entry, 10 ms [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10; @corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].
3. Hold right click (the macro) and left click (Keen Strike) together; press key 1 a few times to open each burst window [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
4. Skills and Stigmas: the same as every PvE setup ([Level 45: set up](/classes/gladiator/setup#pve-skills-to-raise)) [!opinion].
:::

**Who it's for:** you want the macro for your damage but to choose the moment of every burst, with one button for all your buffs. This is aion2-meta's in-game macro setup [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].

The Gladiator's damage is Overhead Slam cancelled into Rending Blow: Rending Blow cuts the end of Overhead Slam's animation, so the next hit lands sooner. Bosses can't be knocked down, so every hit has a 7% chance to make Overhead Slam usable; the more hits, the more Overhead Slams [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].

## The setup

::::play
- **Macro key (one slot, from the bottom):** Overhead Slam, then Rending Blow. That slot is the macro's only entry, 10 ms, on right click; hold it with left click (Keen Strike), whose hits fill the gaps and restore MP [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
- **Buff button (one slot, from the bottom):** Zikel's Blessing, Lunge Stance, Rage Burst. Press it at the start of each burst window; each press fires the first one that is ready, so a few presses open the window [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
- **By hand:** Ruinous Blow every time it is ready; Focused Block and Defiance when you need them; Leaping Slam and Rush Strike to close the gap. **Leave out** Sword Aura Rampage, which only hits staggered targets [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
::::

## Things to know

- Each skill preset has its own macro, and only one runs at a time; switch presets with **Alt + Num4, Num5, Num6** [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
- A slot holds up to four skills; the bottom one fires first [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10; @corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].
- **Skill Queue** is on by default; leave it on. If your basic attack stops coming out while you hold the macro and your MP drops, turn it off [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
- Use only the game's own macro: aion2-meta warns that NCSOFT bans accounts for mouse and third-party macros [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
