1. Make a separate skill preset for this setup [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10; !opinion].
2. Stigmas: **Lunge Stance**, **Zikel's Blessing**, **Rage Burst**, **Focused Block** [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
3. Skills: raise them as in the table below (PvP Specializations differ from PvE) [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
4. Put the skills on the keys as in the bar, and make a macro with only the blue keys [!opinion].
5. Wear the Fierce Battle Amulet (+20% PvP Damage Boost) [@gamingtools-item-database-2026-10].

**Who it's for:** What top Korean PvP players run: the same four Stigmas as PvE (99%+), with more points in Ruinous Blow, Crushing Wave, Rush Strike and Defiance [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10]. Rage Burst means Overhead Slam doesn't need a Knockdown [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].

## Skills and milestones

- Push **Ruinous Blow**, **Crushing Wave** and **Overhead Slam** to 20 [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
- Raise **Rush Strike** (67% at 20+ in PvP vs 7% in PvE) and **Defiance** (40% vs 1%) [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
- Korea has six Stigma slots; their extra two are Tenaciousness (98.5%) and Armor of Balance (90.8%). Swap one in for Zikel's Blessing when you need it [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10; !opinion].

## The macro

Add only the blue keys, in order, delay 10 ms. Keep control, openers and defensive skills out of it so you can time them: creators agree on keeping defensive and mobility skills manual [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10; @fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].

## How to play

::::play
1. Buff (1, 2) out of their range, close in with Q [!opinion].
2. Hold the macro: Rage Burst, then Overhead Slam and Rending Blow (key 5), with Ruinous Blow and Crushing Wave (key 6) [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10; !opinion].
3. Focused Block (E) on their big hit; Mocking Blade (3) and Aerial Snare (7) for control [@gamingtools-skill-focused-block-2026-10; !opinion].
4. Defiance (4) only on the control that leads into a kill [@fextralife-gladiator-builds-2026-10].
::::
