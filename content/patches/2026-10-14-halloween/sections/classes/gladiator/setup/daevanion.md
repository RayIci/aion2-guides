:::quick{title="How to fill your boards"}
1. Your Daevanion boards are **shared by every skill preset**, so you pick one plan for the character, not one per setup [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
2. Open the boards as they unlock: **Nezekan** (level 12), **Zikel** (20), **Vaizel** (30), **Triniel** (40). Each of these four adds one level to every active skill [@corpus-gladiator-daevanion-2026-10; @aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
3. **Plan A** gives the exact nodes for the 136 points you have at 45; **Plan B** numbers every node in the order to open it, so you can follow it point by point [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10; @aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
4. **For PvP:** once the first four are where you want them, put further points into **Azphel** (opens at 45; PvP stats) in Plan B's order [@corpus-gladiator-daevanion-2026-10; @aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].
5. Hover a node to see what it gives, or open the list under each board (on a phone, use the list) [!opinion].
:::

You get 4 Daevanion points a level from level 12: 136 by level 45 [@gamingtools-character-levels-2026-10]. The board costs are 1 point for a small stat, 2 for a passive, 3 for a skill +1 and 4 for a big stat [@aion2meta-gladiator-2026-10].

## What creators say

Each of the first four boards (Nezekan, Zikel, Vaizel, Triniel) adds one level to every Gladiator active skill. Take the **Overhead Slam** and **Rending Blow** nodes first, then Ruinous Blow and Sword Aura Rampage, plus the passive nodes for Experienced Counterstrike, Attack Preparation, Impact Hit and Murderous Burst [@corpus-gladiator-daevanion-2026-10].

- **Orange stat nodes:** start Nezekan with Combat Speed and Zikel with Damage Boost; leave Vaizel's Critical Damage and Triniel's Multi-Hit for later (aLuckyRO) [@corpus-gladiator-daevanion-2026-10].
- gaming.tools spends the 136 points at 45 as Nezekan 43, Zikel 10, Vaizel 13, Triniel 22 and saves the rest for Multi-Hit on Triniel [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
- **Azphel** (opens at 45) has only PvP stats: fill the first four boards first [@corpus-gladiator-daevanion-2026-10].
