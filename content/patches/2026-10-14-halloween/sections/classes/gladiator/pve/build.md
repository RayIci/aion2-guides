Three level-45 presets from gaming.tools' Gladiator endgame build, one per job: **Solo Farm**, **Solo Single Target** and **Group DPS** [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10]. All three share the same Daevanion boards; each preset has the full 203 Skill Points and 30 Stigma points (the 30 includes the Ascension quest's Stigma Shard) [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10; @gamingtools-character-levels-2026-10].

- **Solo Farm:** Knock enemies down and spread Overhead Slam across the pack. Use Mocking Blade and Crushing Wave to keep pressure on nearby enemies. [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10]
- **Solo Single Target:** Keep your main attack loop on a tough enemy and use Mocking Blade to recover health. Save your parry for its heavy attacks. [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10]
- **Group DPS:** Fight close to the boss and use your burst windows for heavy hits. Keep basic attacks available for MP, and save your parry for attacks you can block. [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10]

## Stigmas

The same four in every preset: **Rage Burst** (level 5), **Lunge Stance** (level 5), **Zikel's Blessing** (level 10) and **Focused Block** (level 5). A Stigma only counts once it sits on a quick slot [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].

## Daevanion

Of the 136 Daevanion points at level 45, the build opens nodes on four boards: Nezekan (43 points), Zikel (10), Vaizel (13) and Triniel (22), and saves the rest toward Multi-Hit Chance on Triniel [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].

This is one site's recommendation. No second source has been checked yet, so treat the exact levels as a starting point [!opinion].
