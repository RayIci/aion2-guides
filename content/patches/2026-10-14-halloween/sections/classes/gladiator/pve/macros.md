## Setup

1. Turn on **Skill Queue** under Settings → Combat → Controls. Without it, steps pressed while a skill is still casting are lost [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
2. Bind **Macro** to the free right mouse button under Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. In AION 1 Control Mode use a keyboard key instead [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
3. Keep your generator on left mouse, out of the macro, and hold both buttons while you fight [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
4. Every step waits 10 ms. Manual input always takes priority over the macro [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
