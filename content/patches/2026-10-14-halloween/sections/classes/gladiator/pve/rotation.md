The Gladiator fights with a held generator (left mouse) plus a macro that presses a fixed list of quick slots. Each quick slot casts the first ready skill in its list, so the lists below are priorities, not a strict order [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].

::::play
## Core loop

- **Knockdowns open Overhead Slam.** Overhead Slam is the hardest hit and needs a valid target state; Rage Burst opens a temporary window for it [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10; @gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].
- **Keep Keen Strike in the loop for MP.** The Keen Strike chain restores 100, 100 and 120 MP [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-wrathful-strike-2026-10].
- **Parry with Focused Block** just before an attack you can block [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
- **Rush Strike right after a dodge** [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].

### Solo Farm

Knock enemies down and spread Overhead Slam across the pack. Use Mocking Blade and Crushing Wave to keep pressure on nearby enemies. [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10]

1. Mocking Blade → Crushing Wave, Rage Burst
2. Overhead Slam → Aerial Snare, Rending Blow
3. Ankle Slice → Ruinous Blow, Leaping Slam
4. Overhead Slam → Aerial Snare, Rending Blow
5. Lunge Stance → Zikel's Blessing, Sword Aura Rampage
6. Mocking Blade → Crushing Wave, Rage Burst
7. Overhead Slam → Aerial Snare, Rending Blow
8. Rush Strike
### Solo Single Target

Keep your main attack loop on a tough enemy and use Mocking Blade to recover health. Save your parry for its heavy attacks. [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10]

1. Mocking Blade → Rage Burst, Crushing Wave
2. Overhead Slam → Aerial Snare, Sword Aura Rampage, Rending Blow
3. Ankle Slice → Ruinous Blow, Leaping Slam
4. Overhead Slam → Aerial Snare, Sword Aura Rampage, Rending Blow
5. Lunge Stance → Zikel's Blessing
6. Overhead Slam → Aerial Snare, Sword Aura Rampage, Rending Blow
7. Rush Strike
### Group DPS

Fight close to the boss and use your burst windows for heavy hits. Keep basic attacks available for MP, and save your parry for attacks you can block. [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10]

1. Lunge Stance → Zikel's Blessing
2. Rage Burst → Mocking Blade, Crushing Wave
3. Overhead Slam → Aerial Snare, Sword Aura Rampage, Rending Blow
4. Rage Burst → Mocking Blade, Crushing Wave
5. Overhead Slam → Aerial Snare, Sword Aura Rampage, Rending Blow
6. Ankle Slice → Ruinous Blow, Leaping Slam
7. Overhead Slam → Aerial Snare, Sword Aura Rampage, Rending Blow
8. Rush Strike
