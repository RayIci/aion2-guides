A weekly solo time trial: you improve your rank by replaying it, the content changes each week, and season rank gives rewards. Open it from Main Menu → Ascension Trial (level 45) [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

| Difficulty | Item level |
|---|---|
| Easy | 1,000 |
| Normal | 1,500 |
| Hard | 2,000 |
| Highest | 2,500 |

[@corpus-item-level-2026-10]

Trial maps in the client: Chamber of the Dead, Depository of Fates, Forgotten Repository, Nightmare Altar, Sanctum of Loathing and Tyrant's Hideout, each with harder versions named Doom, Hell or Insane [@gamingtools-ascension-trial-list-2026-10].

Use the **Solo Single Target** preset for bosses and **Solo Farm** for packs [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
