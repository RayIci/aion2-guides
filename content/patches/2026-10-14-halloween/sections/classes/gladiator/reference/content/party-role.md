The Gladiator is tagged Tank and DPS: usually a frontline damage dealer that can be a second tank thanks to its HP [@gamingtools-gladiator-2026-10]. Its basic attacks raise Enmity (threat), so it can pull aggro off the real tank [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-rending-blow-2026-10].

In a party, gaming.tools' **Group DPS** preset says: fight close to the boss, use burst windows for heavy hits, keep basic attacks for MP and save the parry for attacks you can block [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].

Which party compositions work best is not written yet [!unverified].
