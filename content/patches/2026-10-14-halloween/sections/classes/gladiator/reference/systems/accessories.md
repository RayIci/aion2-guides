- **Noble Belt** and **Revelation Amulet** at Unique are 65 item level each: the belt from Stronghold scrolls, the amulet from feather scrolls [@corpus-item-level-2026-10; @raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10].
- Quest jewelry such as the **Liberator Bracelet** is item level 43 [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
- The 4th Ascension quest gives an **Ascension Bracelet** and a **Fierce Battle Amulet** [@gamingtools-ascension-rewards-2026-10].
- Conquest ceiling accessories: **Berk** (Krao Cave), **Aulamus** (Urugugu Canyon) and **Enraged Kromede** (Fire Temple) necklace, earrings and ring [@gamingtools-exp-krao-cave-conquest-2026-10; @gamingtools-exp-urugugu-canyon-conquest-2026-10; @gamingtools-exp-fire-temple-conquest-2026-10].
- Order after 1,400: Vakron armour first, Urugugu accessories next, then Fire Temple [@raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10].
- On the crafting route, craft a necklace, two earrings and two rings before the weapon [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
