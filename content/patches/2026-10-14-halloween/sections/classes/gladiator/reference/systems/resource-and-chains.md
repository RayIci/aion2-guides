The Gladiator has no class resource of its own: its skill resource is **MP** [@gamingtools-gladiator-2026-10].

## Skills that restore MP

- **Keen Strike**: Restores 100 MP [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10]
- **Rupture Strike**: Restores 100 MP [@gamingtools-skill-rupture-strike-2026-10]
- **Wrathful Strike**: Restores 120 MP [@gamingtools-skill-wrathful-strike-2026-10]
- **Focused Block**: Restores 110 MP [@gamingtools-skill-focused-block-2026-10]
- **Reckless Strike**: Restores 150 MP [@gamingtools-skill-reckless-strike-2026-10]

## Chains

Opening skills lead into chain skills, used right after them:

- **Rending Blow** → **Smashing Blow** [@gamingtools-skill-rending-blow-2026-10]
- **Keen Strike** → **Rupture Strike** [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10]
- **Rupture Strike** → **Wrathful Strike** [@gamingtools-skill-rupture-strike-2026-10]
- **Crushing Wave** → **Frenzied Wave** [@gamingtools-skill-crushing-wave-2026-10]
- **Blade Toss** → **Blade Dance** [@gamingtools-skill-blade-toss-2026-10]

**Reckless Strike** is added as a chain skill by a Keen Strike Specialization at skill level 16 [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10].
