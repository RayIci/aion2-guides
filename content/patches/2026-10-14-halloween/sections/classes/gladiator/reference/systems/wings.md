Daevas can change wings and fly. The Ascension quest in Poeta or Ishalgen gives your first wings and unlocks flight [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

## Wings from Ascension quests

| Quest | Wings |
|---|---|
| Opening the Way to a New Ascension | Intermediate Daeva Wings |
| Pursuing the Arch Daeva Dream | Superior Daeva Wings |
| Receiving the Empyrean Lords' Might | Ultimate Daeva Wings |
| Ascension Grade 6 | Fledgling Arch Daeva Wings |

[@gamingtools-ascension-rewards-2026-10]

## Flight Power

The 9 Oct 2026 maintenance raised the Flight Power of the four quest wings by 500 each: Daeva's Lesser Wings 2,000 → 2,500, and Daeva's Intermediate, Superior and Ultimate Wings 500 → 1,000 [@nc-global-maint-2026-10-09].

Wing stats beyond Flight Power are not written yet [!unverified].
