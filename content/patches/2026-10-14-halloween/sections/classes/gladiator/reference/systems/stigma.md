Stigma skills are strong class-specific skills that you switch depending on the situation, such as PvP or boss fights [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

## Slots and Shards

| Character level | Stigma slots | Stigma points |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | 1 | – |
| 27 | 2 | 5 |
| 32 | 3 | 10 |
| 37 | 4 | 15 |
| 45 | 4 | 29 |

[@gamingtools-character-levels-2026-10]

- Stigma skills are learned at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension”, which also gives a Stigma Shard [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10; @gamingtools-ascension-rewards-2026-10].
- Levels are raised with Stigma Shards: 1 Shard per level for levels 1–5, 2 for 6–10, 4 for 11–15 and 8 for 16–20 [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10].
- Max Stigma skill level is 25 [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10].
