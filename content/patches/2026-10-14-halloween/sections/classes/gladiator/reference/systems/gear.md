## By stage

- **Leveling:** equip quest upgrades as they come [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-leveling-2026-10].
- **Level 45:** the main story gives a Unique weapon; the last Ascension and Abyss quests give a guard and a bracelet [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
- **1,400 → 2,100:** Unique armor at item level 70 from Vakron Sky Island chests, or crafted accessories and weapon [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
- **2,100+:** the Fire Temple weapon [@raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10] and Nuakum armor (item level 86) from Ferocious Horn Den, which one creator calls the best non-crafted gear on Global in Season 1 [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
- **Crafted line:** True Dragon Lord (Elyos) or Star Dragon Lord (Asmodians) starts at item level 62 and upgrades in place 62 → 70 → 78 → 86 → 94 → 102 [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
- There is no Heroic gear in Global Season 1 [@playnews-level-cap-2026-09].
