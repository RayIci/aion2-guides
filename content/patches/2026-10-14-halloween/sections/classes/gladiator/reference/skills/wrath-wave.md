**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-wrath-wave-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 1068 + 175.5% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 5m of the caster and has a 50% chance to inflict Knockdown for 3s. 100% chance to inflict Knockdown on NPC targets. 50 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-wrath-wave-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Circle 5 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cooldown | 60s |
| Cost | 200 MP |
| Max skill level | 25 |

### Specializations

- Up to +20% damage when more targets hit (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-wrath-wave-2026-10]
- Reduces Attack by 20% for 5s (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-wrath-wave-2026-10]
- Deals damage 1 additional time (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-wrath-wave-2026-10]
- +25% Knockdown Chance (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-wrath-wave-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-wrath-wave-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-wrath-wave-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build. PvP: [PvP](/classes/gladiator/pvp).
