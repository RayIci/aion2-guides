**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-assault-strike-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Selects a target within 20m and deals 998 + 164% Attack damage up to 4 enemies within 4m of the target, with a 30% chance to inflict Knockdown for 3s. 100% chance to inflict Knockdown on NPC targets. Increases Defense by up to 20% proportional to the number of targets hit. [@gamingtools-skill-assault-strike-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 20 m |
| Target | Enemies, Circle 4 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cooldown | 120s |
| Max skill level | 25 |

### Specializations

- +20% Attack for 5s on hit (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assault-strike-2026-10]
- 500 MP damage on hit (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assault-strike-2026-10]
- +10m range (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assault-strike-2026-10]
- +20% Knockdown Chance (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assault-strike-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-assault-strike-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assault-strike-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build. PvP: [PvP](/classes/gladiator/pvp).
