**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-blade-toss-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 534 + 87.75% Attack damage to a target within 20m and reduces Defense by 30% and Incoming Heal by 50% for 10s. 5 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-blade-toss-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 20 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 20s |
| Max skill level | 25 |
| Chain role | Opening Skill |
| Chains into | Blade Dance |

### Specializations

- +20% **Blade Toss** Incoming Heal reduction (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-blade-toss-2026-10]
- **Blade Toss** inflicts Slow for 5s (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-blade-toss-2026-10]
- **Blade Toss** breaks Protective Shields (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-blade-toss-2026-10]
- **Blade Dance** has 30% chance to inflict Knockdown for 3s (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-blade-toss-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-blade-toss-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-blade-toss-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build. PvP: [PvP](/classes/gladiator/pvp).
