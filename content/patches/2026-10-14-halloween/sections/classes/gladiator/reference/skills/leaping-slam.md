**Active skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-leaping-slam-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Leaps to a target within 20m and deals 70 + 115% Attack damage. 10 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-leaping-slam-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 20 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 15s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-leaping-slam-2026-10].

- Prepare for Battle effect on the caster for 3s (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-leaping-slam-2026-10]
- 50% chance to trigger **Ankle Slice** on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-leaping-slam-2026-10]
- Resets cooldown on defeating an enemy (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-leaping-slam-2026-10]
- +20% PvE Damage Tolerance and +10 PvP Damage Tolerance for 3s (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-leaping-slam-2026-10]
- Changes to AoE skill and pulls in enemies (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-leaping-slam-2026-10]
