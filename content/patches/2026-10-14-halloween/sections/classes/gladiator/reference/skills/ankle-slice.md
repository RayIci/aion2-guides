**Active skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-ankle-slice-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Selects a target within 4m and deals 294 + 150% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 4m with a 50% chance to inflict Root for 3s on Block. 100% chance to inflict Root on NPC targets. 10 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-ankle-slice-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Circle 4 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cooldown | 10s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-ankle-slice-2026-10].

- 30% chance to inflict Knockdown for 3s on landing **Ankle Slice** (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-ankle-slice-2026-10]
- Adds **Ankle Smash** Chain Skill (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-ankle-slice-2026-10]
- +16m **Ankle Slice** range (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-ankle-slice-2026-10]
- Ignores Block and Evasion and lands as Multi-Hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-ankle-slice-2026-10]
- 25% chance to reset **Ankle Slice** cooldown on Block (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-ankle-slice-2026-10]
