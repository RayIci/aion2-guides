**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-zikels-blessing-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Increases the caster's Attack by 20% and Accuracy by 100 for 10s. [@gamingtools-skill-zikels-blessing-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Cooldown | 120s |
| Max skill level | 25 |

### Specializations

- +50% Stagger Gauge Damage (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-zikels-blessing-2026-10]
- +10% PvE Damage Boost and +5% PvP Damage Boost (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-zikels-blessing-2026-10]
- +50% Multi-Hit chance (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-zikels-blessing-2026-10]
- +20% Status Effect Chance (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-zikels-blessing-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-zikels-blessing-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-zikels-blessing-2026-10].

In gaming.tools' PvE build: Group DPS (Stigma, level 10), Solo Farm (Stigma, level 10), Solo Single Target (Stigma, level 10) [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10]. Where it goes on your bar: [Level 45: set up](/classes/gladiator/setup). PvP: [PvP](/classes/gladiator/pvp).

**On your skill bar:** key 5, middle slot, pressed by the macro, in all three PvE presets [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10]. Full bar: [Level 45: set up](/classes/gladiator/setup#1-your-skill-bar).
