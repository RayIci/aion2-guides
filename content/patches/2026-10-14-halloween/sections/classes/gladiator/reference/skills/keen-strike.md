**Active skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 51 + 43% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 4m and increases Enmity. Restores 100 MP. [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 5.9 m, up to 4 targets |
| Max skill level | 40 |
| Chain role | Opening Skill |
| Chains into | Rupture Strike |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10].

- +20% MP restored (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10]
- Absorbs 1% HP (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10]
- +50% Multi-Hit on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10]
- -1s **Ruinous Blow** cooldown on hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10]
- Adds **Reckless Strike** Chain Skill (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10]
