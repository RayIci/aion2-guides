**Passive skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-blood-absorption-2026-10].

The caster has a 50% chance to restore an amount of HP and 1.6% Max HP on landing an attack. Immediately restores 26% Max HP when HP drops to 50% or less. Cooldown: 1s Instant Healing Cooldown: 60s (the data page does not show the HP amount for this level) [@gamingtools-skill-blood-absorption-2026-10]
