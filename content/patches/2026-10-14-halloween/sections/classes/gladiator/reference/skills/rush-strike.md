**Active skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-rush-strike-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Moves to a target within 10m and deals 330 + 125% Attack damage with a 30% chance to inflict Knockdown for 3s after using **Dodge** or while flying. 100% chance to inflict Knockdown on NPC targets. 10 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-rush-strike-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 10 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 20s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-rush-strike-2026-10].

- Restores 300 Stamina on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-rush-strike-2026-10]
- +10m range (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-rush-strike-2026-10]
- Changes to Charge Skill and deals AoE damage (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-rush-strike-2026-10]
- -10s cooldown (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-rush-strike-2026-10]
- +25% Knockdown Chance (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-rush-strike-2026-10]
