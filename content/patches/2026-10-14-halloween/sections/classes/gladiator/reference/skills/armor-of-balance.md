**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Increases Impact-type Resist by 50% for 10s. [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Cooldown | 90s |
| Max skill level | 25 |

### Specializations

- +20% Ailment-type and Mental-type Resist (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10]
- x1.5 **Blood Absorption** effect for the duration (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10]
- x1.5 **Protection Armor** effect for the duration (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10]
- +50% additional Impact-type Resist (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build. PvP: [PvP](/classes/gladiator/pvp).
