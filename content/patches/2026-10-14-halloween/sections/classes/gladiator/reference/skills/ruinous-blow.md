**Active skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-ruinous-blow-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Rushes to a target within 7.5m to deal 2387 + 689% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies nearby and grants Prepare for Battle for 20s. Prepare for Battle: Increases PvE Damage Boost by 20%, PvP Damage Boost by 10%, Critical Hit by 100, and Status Effect Chance by 15% 25 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-ruinous-blow-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 7.5 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 9 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cooldown | 45s |
| Cost | 250 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-ruinous-blow-2026-10].

- +20% Skill Speed (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-ruinous-blow-2026-10]
- +12.5m Range (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-ruinous-blow-2026-10]
- +30% Skill Critical Hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-ruinous-blow-2026-10]
- Extra damage on hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-ruinous-blow-2026-10]
- Ignores Block and Evasion and lands as Multi-Hit (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-ruinous-blow-2026-10]
