**Active skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 207 + 194.4% Attack damage to a target within 4m and inflicts Knockdown for 3s. Changes skill motion when attacking targets with Incapacitated Immunity. 15 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 20s |
| Cost | 200 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10].

- Absorbs 4% HP, absorbs 15% HP of target with Incapacitated Immunity (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10]
- Changes to AoE damage on down strike (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10]
- Adds 2 additional strikes (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10]
- +1s Knockdown duration (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10]
- Ignores Block and Evasion and lands as Multi-Hit (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10]
