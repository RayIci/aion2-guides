**Stigma skill** for the Greatsword [@gamingtools-skill-forced-restraint-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 1059 + 174% Attack damage to a target within 4m with a 75% chance to inflict Seal for 5s. 100% chance to inflict Seal on NPC targets. 20 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-forced-restraint-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 60s |
| Cost | 100 MP |
| Max skill level | 25 |

### Specializations

- 50% chance to inflict Root for 5s (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-forced-restraint-2026-10]
- -1,000 Stamina (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-forced-restraint-2026-10]
- +2s Seal duration (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-forced-restraint-2026-10]
- +25% Seal chance (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-forced-restraint-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Opening the Way to a New Ascension” [@gamingtools-skill-forced-restraint-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-forced-restraint-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build. PvP: [PvP](/classes/gladiator/pvp).
