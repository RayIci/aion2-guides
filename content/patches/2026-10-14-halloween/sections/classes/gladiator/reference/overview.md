The Gladiator is a melee class with a greatsword: usually a frontline damage dealer that can also act as a second tank thanks to its high HP [@gamingtools-gladiator-2026-10]. The game tags it as both **Tank** and **DPS**, and it is open to both Elyos and Asmodians [@gamingtools-gladiator-2026-10].

## What it plays like

- **Area damage up close.** Most of its basic skills hit up to 4 enemies in front of it within about 4 m, like Keen Strike and Rending Blow [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-rending-blow-2026-10].
- **No class resource.** It spends and restores MP. The Keen Strike chain refills it: Keen Strike and Rupture Strike restore 100 MP each and Wrathful Strike 120 [@gamingtools-gladiator-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-rupture-strike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-wrathful-strike-2026-10].
- **Chains.** Opening skills lead into chain skills, so the rotation is built from short chains (Keen Strike → Rupture Strike → Wrathful Strike) [@gamingtools-skill-keen-strike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-rupture-strike-2026-10].
- **Tough.** Passives add HP, Block and damage tolerance, like Survival Stance and Protection Armor [@gamingtools-skill-survival-stance-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-protection-armor-2026-10].

## In this patch

The 14 Oct 2026 patch made no Gladiator balance changes [@nc-global-notes-2026-10-14].

Creators most often name Gladiator, Assassin and Templar for 1v1 and small fights [@corpus-best-pvp-class-2026-10]. Outside the Battlefield gear matters more than class [@corpus-best-pvp-class-2026-10].
