**Gladiator**: Tank, DPS, uses a Greatsword [@gamingtools-classes-2026-10; @nc-global-skins-2026-10].

A damage race: break its first control with Defiance only if it leads into a kill, keep Focused Block for its biggest hit, and land Mocking Blade → Overhead Slam first [!opinion].

## The mirror

The other Gladiator has the same tools: Mocking Blade's guaranteed knockdown, Defiance, Focused Block, and Stigmas such as Forced Restraint (Seal) and Tenaciousness [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-defiance-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-focused-block-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-forced-restraint-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-tenaciousness-2026-10].

- Its Defiance has a 60s cooldown: bait it with a knockdown, then chain your real one after it is spent [@gamingtools-skill-defiance-2026-10; !opinion].
- Forced Restraint's Seal can't be broken by Defiance; keep a Healing Potion ready [@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10].

This class's own skills and crowd control are not researched yet, so there are no specific counters here [!unverified].
