**Assassin**: DPS, uses a Dagger [@gamingtools-classes-2026-10; @nc-global-skins-2026-10].

A damage race: break its first control with Defiance only if it leads into a kill, keep Focused Block for its biggest hit, and land Mocking Blade → Overhead Slam first [!opinion].

## Its kit

The Assassin opens with Shadowstrike, which moves behind you and always stuns for 3s, then Shadow Fall, which knocks a stunned target down for 3s [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10]. Its burst comes from behind: Ambush and Triniel's Dagger hit 30% harder there [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10]. It escapes with Evasion Stance, Shadowstep (3s Stealth) and Shadow Walk (20s Stealth out of combat) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10].

- Keep Defiance for Shadowstrike's Stun, so Shadow Fall has nothing to follow up on [@gamingtools-skill-defiance-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10; !opinion].
- Turn to face it: its biggest hits need your back [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10; !opinion].
- Don't swing into Evasion Stance, which evades everything for a moment [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10].
