## Crowd control and how to break it

| Effect | Type | What it does | Removed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stun | Impact | immobilized and incapacitated | Defiance or cleansing |
| Knockdown | Impact | immobilized and incapacitated | Defiance or cleansing |
| Airborne | Impact | immobilized and incapacitated | Defiance or cleansing |
| Grab | Impact | immobilized and incapacitated | Defiance or cleansing |
| Fear | Mental | forced to move away from the caster | Defiance or cleansing |
| Taunt | Mental | forced to move to and attack the caster | Defiance or cleansing |
| Frost | Ailment | immobilized and incapacitated; broken by Frost Burst | Defiance or cleansing |
| Sleep | Mental | immobilized and incapacitated; may break when hit | cleansing |
| Polymorph | Mental | immobilized and incapacitated; may break when hit | cleansing |
| Paralyze | Ailment | immobilized and incapacitated | cleansing |
| Seal | Mental | cannot use skills | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Root | Ailment | immobilized | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Blind | Ailment | more attacks fail | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Lethargy | Ailment | lower Combat Speed | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Slow | Ailment | lower Move Speed | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Poison | Ailment | damage over time, less potion recovery and healing | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Bleed | Ailment | damage over time | Healing Potions or cleansing |

[@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10]

**Defiance** is the Gladiator's own break: it removes Stun, Knockdown, Airborne, Grab, Frost and Fear and then grants Tenacity (status-effect immunity) for 5s [@gamingtools-skill-defiance-2026-10]. The crowd-control table also lists Taunt as removable by Defiance [@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10]. Sleep, Polymorph, Paralyze, Seal and Root need cleansing or potions instead [@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10]. Its cooldown is 60s, so spend it on the stun or knockdown that leads into a kill, not the first one you take [@gamingtools-skill-defiance-2026-10; !opinion].

## Gladiator tools for PvP

- **Armor of Balance** (Stigma): Impact-type Resist +50% for 10s, 90s cooldown [@gamingtools-skill-armor-of-balance-2026-10].
- **Focused Block** (Stigma): guarantees a Parry and doubles Block damage reduction for a moment, restoring 110 MP and Stamina on the parry; use it just before an attack you can block [@gamingtools-skill-focused-block-2026-10; @gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].
- **Blade Toss** (Stigma): 20 m ranged hit that cuts Defense by 30% and Incoming Heal by 50% for 10s [@gamingtools-skill-blade-toss-2026-10].
- **Aerial Snare** and **Ankle Slice** for catching targets; see their skill pages for exact effects [@gamingtools-skill-aerial-snare-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-ankle-slice-2026-10].

## PvP stats

Willpower raises Status Effect Resist and Dexterity raises Critical Hit Resist; Zikel's board adds Perfect Resist [@gamingtools-systems-stat-conversion-2026-10]. PvP Damage Tolerance comes from passives such as Survival Stance [@gamingtools-skill-survival-stance-2026-10].

Positioning and team play per mode are on each mode's tactics page.
