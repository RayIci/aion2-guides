The Abyss (Reshanta) is open faction-vs-faction PvP from level 45 (item level 1,000): Chaotic Lower, Middle and Upper Reshanta [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10; @gamingtools-abyss-2026-10; @corpus-item-level-2026-10]. Stored entry time is capped at 7 hours [@nc-global-notes-2026-10-14], and Abyss points reset Wednesday 16:00 [@gamingtools-systems-weekly-resets-2026-10].

- Large fights favour your AoE: Wrath Wave, Crushing Wave and Lifestealing Blade all hit up to 4 enemies [!opinion].
- Your knockdown chain only works at 4 m; close the gap with Leaping Slam (20 m), Rush Strike (10 m) or Fracturing Rush (10 m) [@gamingtools-skill-leaping-slam-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-rush-strike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-fracturing-rush-2026-10].
- Watch for Seal and Root: keep a Healing Potion ready, since Defiance can't break them [@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10].

More detailed tactics need vetted PvP sources [!unverified].
