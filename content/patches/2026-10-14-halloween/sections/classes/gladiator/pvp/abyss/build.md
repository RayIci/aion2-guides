No vetted Gladiator PvP build source exists yet, so the picks below are worked out from the skill data [!opinion].

Keep the skill levels and Specializations from PvE → PvE build, and change the Stigmas: the game expects you to switch Stigmas between situations such as PvP and boss fights [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10], and skill presets keep a separate PvP loadout [@gamingtools-build-gladiator-pve-2026-10].

Suggested 4 Stigmas: **Wrath Wave**, **Armor of Balance**, **Tenaciousness**, **Rage Burst**. Bring **Blade Toss** when the other team has a healer you can reach [!opinion].
