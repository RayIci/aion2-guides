10v10 Relic cart on Odyle Shard Cradle, 10 minutes, 10s respawn, solo queue only. Participation: 1,000 Abyss Points and a Silver Medal of Merit; win: 3,000 and 2 medals (3 rewarded matches each per week). Open daily 11:00–14:00 and 19:00–21:00 [@gamingtools-arena-tactics-2026-10].

- The cart is the objective: Fracturing Rush and Wrath Wave move and knock down the players around it [!opinion].
- Your knockdown chain only works at 4 m; close the gap with Leaping Slam (20 m), Rush Strike (10 m) or Fracturing Rush (10 m) [@gamingtools-skill-leaping-slam-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-rush-strike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-fracturing-rush-2026-10].
- Watch for Seal and Root: keep a Healing Potion ready, since Defiance can't break them [@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10].
