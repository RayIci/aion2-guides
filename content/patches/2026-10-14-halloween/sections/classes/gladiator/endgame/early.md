## PvP

The **Arena** (1v1 and 5v5) and the **Battlefield** open at 45; the Battlefield is equalized, so play it now for Abyss Points [@corpus-arena-guide-2026-10; @corpus-battlefield-guide-2026-10]. The **Abyss** opens at item level 1,000; its Abyss Points buy Stigma Shards [@corpus-abyss-guide-2026-10]. See [PvP](/classes/gladiator/pvp).

Level 45 to item level **1,400**. Most players hit 45 at 850–950 item level, depending on side quests [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].

## What opens

- **700:** Krao Cave Conquest, Draupnir Exploration and Conquest [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
- **1,000:** the Abyss (Chaotic Lower Reshanta) and Ascension Trial on Easy [@corpus-item-level-2026-10; @raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10].

## How to reach 1,400

::::play
1. Do the two quests that reward a **Clash Rune** chest: two runes at 40 item level each take you to about 1,000 [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
2. Finish the **main story**: it gives a Unique weapon; the last Ascension and Abyss quests give a guard and a bracelet [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
3. Do every green **side quest** and **Sealed Dungeon** on your faction's map, for Daevanion points [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
4. **Strongholds** for belt scrolls and **feathers** for amulet scrolls: make the **Noble Belt** and **Revelation Amulet** Unique (65 item level each) [@corpus-item-level-2026-10; @raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10].
5. Both Clash Runes to **+1**: +1 always succeeds, +2 has an 80% chance and a failure can destroy the rune [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
6. Weapon, guard and bracelet to **+10**, with one grey or green Manastone line on each piece [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
7. Don't enhance gear you'll replace at the next dungeon [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
