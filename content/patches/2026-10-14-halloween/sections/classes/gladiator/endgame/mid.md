Item level **1,400 to 2,100**, the "energy phase" [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].

## What opens

- **1,400:** Vakron Sky Island (Exploration and Conquest) and Urugugu Canyon Conquest [@corpus-item-level-2026-10; @raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10].
- **1,500:** Ascension Trial Normal; **2,000:** Hard [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
- **1,600:** Transcendence stage 1; **1,900:** stage 2 [@corpus-item-level-2026-10; @raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10].

## Two routes

:::conflict{topic="Where to spend Odyle Energy from 1,400"}
- **Farm armor:** put all Odyle Energy into Vakron Sky Island for Unique armor (item level 70). Run Exploration three times for its guaranteed chest, then repeat Conquest; Vakron Conquest's pity is 21 cube openings. Each new piece to +5, all to +7 once the set is done, Manastones, a Theostone on weapon and guard [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
- **Alts and crafting:** save energy on your main and run lower Expeditions on alts, moving Kina and materials to the main through server storage. Craft a necklace, two earrings, two rings, then the weapon: the first crafted tier (True Dragon Lord for Elyos, Star Dragon Lord for Asmodians) starts at item level 62 and upgrades in place 62 → 70 → 78 → 86 → 94 → 102 [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].

Both routes fill all five Arcana slots in Transcendence stage 1 at 1,600 and move to stage 2 at 1,900 [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
:::

## Voucher crafts

- **Vakron Guard:** 2 Vakron vouchers + 1,500,000 Kina in Substance Morph, 100% success [@raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10].
- **Vakron armour selection chest:** 1 Vakron voucher + 500,000 Kina [@raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10].
- An off-class Aulamus weapon + 2 Urugugu vouchers + 1,500,000 Kina make a chest for your class's item-level-70 weapon [@raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10].
- For the same 40 Odyle, Urugugu Canyon and Vakron Sky Island pay 300,000 Kina, 50% more than Draupnir and Krao Cave's 200,000 [@raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10].
