## The Gladiator in one minute

- A greatsword melee class that counts as a **tank**: in a party without a Templar you hold the boss, and you hold it by dealing damage, since it has no taunt [@corpus-gladiator-guide-2026-10].
- Most of your damage comes from **Overhead Slam**; Rage Burst opens a window where you can use it again and again [@corpus-gladiator-guide-2026-10; @corpus-gladiator-pve-build-2026-10].
- Stand **in front** of the boss: you gain Front Attack Damage Boost, and blocking a hit buffs your whole party [@corpus-gladiator-guide-2026-10].
- Creators most often name Gladiator, Assassin and Templar for 1v1 and small fights [@corpus-best-pvp-class-2026-10].
