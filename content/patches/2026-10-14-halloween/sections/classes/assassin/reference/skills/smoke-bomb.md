**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-smoke-bomb-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 966 + 180% Attack to a target within 10m and inflicts Blind for 5s. 15 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-smoke-bomb-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 10 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 30s |
| Cost | 100 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- +10m range (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-smoke-bomb-2026-10]
- Changes to AoE Skill (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-smoke-bomb-2026-10]
- Inflicts Slow for 5s on hit (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-smoke-bomb-2026-10]
- +1 consecutive use (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-smoke-bomb-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-smoke-bomb-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-smoke-bomb-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build. PvP: [PvP](/classes/assassin/pvp).
