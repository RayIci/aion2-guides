**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Moves to a target afflicted with Stun within 10m, deals 465 + 204% Attack damage, and inflicts Knockdown for 3s. Has a 5% chance to trigger on attacking a target with Incapacitated Immunity. 10 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 10 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 20s |
| Cost | 100 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10].

- Absorbs 10% HP (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10]
- Engraves 3 Insignias for 10s on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10]
- -5s cooldown (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10]
- +1s Knockdown duration (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10]
- Can be used on targets afflicted with Blind (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10]
