**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 126 + 138% Attack damage to a target within 4m. Increases damage by 30% on landing as a Back attack. 7 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 5.9 m |
| Cooldown | 10s |
| Cost | 100 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10].

- Engraves 2 Insignias for 10s on landing as a Back attack (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10]
- Absorbs 3% HP (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10]
- Breaks Protective Shields on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10]
- 50% chance to trigger **Whirlwind Slice** on hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10]
- +2 consecutive uses (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10]
