**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-whirlwind-slice-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 300 + 177% Attack damage to a target within 4m with a 40% chance to rotate and inflict Stun for 3s on Evasion. 100% to inflict Stun on NPC targets. 7 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-whirlwind-slice-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 5.9 m |
| Cooldown | 10s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-whirlwind-slice-2026-10].

- +50% Multi-Hit on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-whirlwind-slice-2026-10]
- Changes to AoE damage (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-whirlwind-slice-2026-10]
- +16m range (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-whirlwind-slice-2026-10]
- +20% Stun Chance (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-whirlwind-slice-2026-10]
- 25% chance to reset **Whirlwind Slice** cooldown on Evasion (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-whirlwind-slice-2026-10]
