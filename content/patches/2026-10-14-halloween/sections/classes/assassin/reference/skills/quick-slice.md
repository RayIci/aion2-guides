**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 61 + 70% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 4m and restores 100 MP. [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 5.9 m, up to 4 targets |
| Max skill level | 40 |
| Chain role | Opening Skill |
| Chains into | Breaking Slice |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10].

- +20% MP restored (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10]
- Absorbs 0.7% HP (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10]
- +50% Multi-Hit on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10]
- -1s **Insignia Explosion** cooldown on hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10]
- Ignores Block and Evasion and lands as a Critical Hit (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned automatically at character level 1, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10].

|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | free |
| 2 | 1 | 1 SP |
| 3 | 4 | 1 SP |
| 4 | 7 | 1 SP |
| 5 | 10 | 2 SP |
| 6 | 13 | 2 SP |
| 7 | 16 | 2 SP |
| 8 | 19 | 4 SP |
| 9 | 22 | 4 SP |

The data page lists learning up to skill level 10; the max skill level is 40 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10].

In gaming.tools' PvE build: Group DPS 10 + 1 bonus, Solo Farm 10 + 1 bonus, Solo Single Target 10 + 1 bonus [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]. Where it goes on your bar: [Level 45: set up](/classes/assassin/setup). PvP: [PvP](/classes/assassin/pvp).
