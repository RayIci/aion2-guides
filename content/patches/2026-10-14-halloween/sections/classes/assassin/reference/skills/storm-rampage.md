**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-storm-rampage-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 107 + 82.5% Attack damage to a target afflicted with Stagger within 4m. [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-storm-rampage-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 5.9 m |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-storm-rampage-2026-10].

- Absorbs 5% HP (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-storm-rampage-2026-10]
- Restores 120 MP on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-storm-rampage-2026-10]
- Changes to mobile skill (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-storm-rampage-2026-10]
- Multi-Hit on hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-storm-rampage-2026-10]
- -1s all skill cooldowns on hit (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-storm-rampage-2026-10]
