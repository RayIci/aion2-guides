**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-contract-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Removes Slow and Root from the caster and increases the caster's Evasion by 200 for 10s. Grants Slow and Root Immunity. Unaffected by cooldown increase effects. [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-contract-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Cooldown | 90s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- 25% chance of Projectile Immunity for the duration (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-contract-2026-10]
- +30% Move Speed for the duration (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-contract-2026-10]
- 50% chance to reduce all skill cooldowns by 1s on Evasion for the duration (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-contract-2026-10]
- +100 Evasion increase (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-contract-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-contract-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-contract-2026-10].
