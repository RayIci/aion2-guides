**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Removes Stun, Knockdown, Airborne, Grab, Frost, and Fear from the caster and grants Tenacity for 5s. Tenacity: Grants Status Effect Immunity Unaffected by cooldown reduction effects. [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Cooldown | 60s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10].

- Adds **Storm Slice** Chain Skill (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10]
- Restores 1000 Stamina on using **Defiance** (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10]
- Restores 20% HP on using **Defiance** (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10]
- +2s Tenacity duration (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10]
- +50% PvE Damage Tolerance and +25% PvP Damage Tolerance for Tenacity duration (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10]
