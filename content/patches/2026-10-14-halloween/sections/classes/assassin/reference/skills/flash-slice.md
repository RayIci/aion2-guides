**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Moves to the opposite side of a target within 4m and deals 181 + 107% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies, with a 40% chance to inflict Blind for 3s. 100% chance to inflict Blind on NPC targets. 7 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Circle 4 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cooldown | 10s |
| Cost | 50 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10].

- +2s Blind duration (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10]
- +6m Range (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10]
- Up to +20% damage when more targets hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10]
- +1 consecutive use (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10]
- 50% chance to rotate and inflict Stun for 3s on hitting a target afflicted with Blind (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10]
