**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-throw-shadowblade-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 778 + 145% Attack damage to a target within 20m and inflicts Slow, which reduces Move Speed by 40% for 5s. 15 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-throw-shadowblade-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 20 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 30s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- Adds **Shadowblade Pursuit** Chain Skill (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-throw-shadowblade-2026-10]
- Resets cooldown on defeating an enemy (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-throw-shadowblade-2026-10]
- Critical Hit on hit (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-throw-shadowblade-2026-10]
- Changes to AoE Skill (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-throw-shadowblade-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-throw-shadowblade-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-throw-shadowblade-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build. PvP: [PvP](/classes/assassin/pvp).
