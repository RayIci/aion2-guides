**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Selects a target within 20m and deals 663 + 220% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 4m, inflicting Stun for 3s. Stun chance is doubled on NPC targets. Damage and Stun chance increase based on Insignia stacks. 1 stack: 742 + 246.4% Attack damage, 10% Stun chance 2 stacks: 822 + 272.8% Attack damage, 20% Stun chance 3 stacks: 902 + 299.2% Attack damage, 30% Stun chance 4 stacks: 981 + 325.6% Attack damage, 40% Stun chance 5 stacks: 1061 + 352% Attack damage, 50% Stun chance 7 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10]

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10].

- Adds rotate effect (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10]
- +50% Multi-Hit on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10]
- Keep 2 stacks of Insignias after **Insignia Explosion** (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10]
- +1s Stun duration (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10]
- -3s cooldown (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10]
