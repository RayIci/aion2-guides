**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-swift-contract-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Increases the caster's Combat Speed by 20% for 10s. [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-swift-contract-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Cooldown | 60s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- -20% MP Cost for the duration (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-swift-contract-2026-10]
- x1.5 **Rear Smite** effect for the duration (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-swift-contract-2026-10]
- x1.5 **Assault Stance** effect for the duration (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-swift-contract-2026-10]
- +10% additional Combat Speed (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-swift-contract-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-swift-contract-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-swift-contract-2026-10].

In gaming.tools' PvE build: Group DPS (Stigma, level 5), Solo Farm (Stigma, level 5), Solo Single Target (Stigma, level 5) [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]. Where it goes on your bar: [Level 45: set up](/classes/assassin/setup). PvP: [PvP](/classes/assassin/pvp).

**On your skill bar:** key 5, bottom slot, tried first, pressed by the macro, in all three PvE presets [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]. Full bar: [Level 45: set up](/classes/assassin/setup/pve-full-macro#your-skill-bar).

**In the other setups:** [PvE B](/classes/assassin/setup/pve-core-macro): key 2, by hand; [PvE C](/classes/assassin/setup/pve-no-macro): key 2, by hand; [PvP 2](/classes/assassin/setup/pvp-ladder): key 2, by hand [!opinion].
