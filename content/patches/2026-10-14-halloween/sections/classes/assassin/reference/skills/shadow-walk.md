**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Grants Stealth for 20s. Cannot be used in combat and the effect is removed upon entering combat. [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Cooldown | 120s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- Critical Hit on attack during Stealth (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10]
- +20% Move Speed for the duration (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10]
- Available in combat (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10]
- Resets cooldown on player kill assists (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build. PvP: [PvP](/classes/assassin/pvp).
