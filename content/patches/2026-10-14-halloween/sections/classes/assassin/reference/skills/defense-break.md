**Passive skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defense-break-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Reduces the target's Defense by 12% and Status Effect Resist by 11% for 10s when landing an attack on a target afflicted with Stagger or an Impact-type status. Cooldown: 20s [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defense-break-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Max skill level | 40 |
