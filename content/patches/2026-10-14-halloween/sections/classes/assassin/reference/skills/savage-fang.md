**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 1074 + 200% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 4m and engraves 5 Insignias for 10s. 50 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 5.9 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cooldown | 60s |
| Cost | 200 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- Up to +20% damage when more targets hit (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10]
- +50 Stagger Gauge Damage (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10]
- +10% PvE Damage Boost and +5% PvP Damage Boost for 10s on hit (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10]
- Critical Hit on hit (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10].
