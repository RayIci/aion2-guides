**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 1374 + 256% Attack damage to a target within 4m and increases all of the target's skill cooldowns by 5% of their duration. Increases damage by 30% if attacking from behind the target. 20 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 45s |
| Cost | 200 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- Changes to mobile skill (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10]
- -10% all skill cooldowns on hit (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10]
- Resets cooldown on defeating an enemy with **Triniel's Dagger** (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10]
- +3% all skill cooldowns (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10].

In gaming.tools' PvE build: Group DPS (Stigma, level 5), Solo Farm (Stigma, level 5), Solo Single Target (Stigma, level 5) [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]. Where it goes on your bar: [Level 45: set up](/classes/assassin/setup). PvP: [PvP](/classes/assassin/pvp).

**On your skill bar:** key 5, top slot, pressed by the macro, in all three PvE presets [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]. Full bar: [Level 45: set up](/classes/assassin/setup#1-your-skill-bar).
