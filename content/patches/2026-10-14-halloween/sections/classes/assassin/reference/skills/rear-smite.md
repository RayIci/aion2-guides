**Passive skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-rear-smite-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Increases the caster's Back Attack Damage Boost by 3%, PvE Damage Boost by 3%, and PvP Damage Boost by 1.5%. [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-rear-smite-2026-10]
