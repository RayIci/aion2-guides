**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Moves behind a target within 10m and deals 239 + 105% Attack damage with a 50% chance to inflict Blind for 3s after using **Dodge** or while flying. 100% chance to inflict Blind on NPC targets. 10 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 10 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 20s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10].

- Restores 300 Stamina on landing **Infiltrate** (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10]
- +10m **Infiltrate** range (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10]
- Breaks Protective Shields on landing **Infiltrate** (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10]
- Adds **Dark Strike** Chain Skill (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10]
- +20% **Infiltrate** Blind Chance (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10]
