The Assassin has no class resource of its own: its skill resource is **MP** [@gamingtools-assassin-2026-10].

## Skills that restore MP

- **Quick Slice**: restores 100 MP [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10]
- **Breaking Slice**: restores 105 MP [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-breaking-slice-2026-10]
- **Swift Slice**: restores 120 MP [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-swift-slice-2026-10]
- **Evasion Stance**: Restores 110 MP [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10]
- **Heart Gore**: restores 100 MP [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10]

## Chains

- **Quick Slice** → **Breaking Slice** [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10]
- **Breaking Slice** → **Swift Slice** [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-breaking-slice-2026-10]
- **Savage Roar** → **Savage Back Kick** [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10]
- **Savage Back Kick** → **Savage Smash** [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-back-kick-2026-10]

## Insignias

Insignias are the Assassin's build-and-spend mechanic: Savage Roar engraves 1 for 10s, Savage Fang engraves 5, and Insignia Explosion's damage and Stun chance grow with them [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10]. Aerial Bind always launches a target with 5 Insignias [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10].
