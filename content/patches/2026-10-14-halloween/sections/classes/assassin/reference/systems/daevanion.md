Daevanion is a growth board that raises stats and skill levels with Daevanion Crystals or Fragments; the 2nd Ascension activates it [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

- The Assassin has five boards: **Nezekan**, **Zikel**, **Vaizel**, **Triniel** and **Azphel** [@gamingtools-assassin-2026-10].
- You earn 4 Daevanion Crystals per level from level 12: 44 by level 22 and 136 by 45 [@gamingtools-character-levels-2026-10].

The leveling build's node order per level is on each Leveling page [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10].
