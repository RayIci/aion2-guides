Arcana are holy relics: grails, parchments, compasses, bells, mirrors and scales. You enhance and refine them to draw out more power, and they come in different grades (from level 45) [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

Arcana come from Transcendence, and equipping them raises your item level [!unverified].
