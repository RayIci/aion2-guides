## How to play

::::play
- Shadowstrike opens, Heart Gore follows your critical hits, and Quick Slice keeps your MP up [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10].
- Hold left mouse (your generator) and the macro key together; use the other skills when needed [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10]. Macro setup: Systems → Macros.

### Macro at level 5

| Step | Quick slot (first ready skill wins) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Shadowstrike → Storm Rampage |
| 2 | Heart Gore → Ambush, Savage Roar |
| 3 | Heart Gore → Ambush, Savage Roar |

## Gear

Equip quest upgrades as you get them. While leveling, aim for +5 on your weapon and guard before spending on other gear [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10].

## Where to level

Poeta (Elyos) or Ishalgen (Asmodians) until level 10 [@fextralife-regions-2026-10].
