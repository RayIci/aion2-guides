## How to play

::::play
- The build's plan, complete from level 22: Savage Roar engraves Insignias that Insignia Explosion cashes in, Heart Gore follows up your critical hits, and Quick Slice keeps your MP up between them. Flash Slice and Shadowstrike put you behind targets for Ambush, and Throw Shadowblade carries you from pack to pack [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10].
- Hold left mouse (your generator) and the macro key together; use the other skills when needed [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10]. Macro setup: Systems → Macros.
- Evasion Stance (Q) right before a big hit [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10].

## Gear

Equip quest upgrades as you get them. While leveling, aim for +5 on your weapon and guard before spending on other gear [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10].
