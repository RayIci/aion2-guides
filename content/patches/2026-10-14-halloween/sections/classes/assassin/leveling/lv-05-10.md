:::quick
1. Level in Poeta (Elyos) or Ishalgen (Asmodians) until level 10 [@fextralife-regions-2026-10].
2. Raise first: **Heart Gore** to 4, **Quick Slice** to 4, **Savage Roar** to 4, **Ambush** to 4, **Whirlwind Slice** to 2 [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10].
3. Set the macro for this bracket (table under How to play), then hold left mouse and the macro key [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10].
4. Tick the checklist at the bottom before you move on [!opinion].
:::

## On arrival

At level 5 the build has Quick Slice 3, Savage Roar 2, Shadowstrike 1, Ambush 1, Heart Gore 1, Storm Rampage 1, Heightened Sixth Sense 1 [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10].

## How to play

::::play
- Shadowstrike opens, Heart Gore follows your critical hits, and Quick Slice keeps your MP up [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10].
- Hold left mouse (your generator) and the macro key together; use the other skills when needed [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10]. Macro setup: Systems → Macros.
- Infiltrate (E) right after a dodge [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10].

### Macro at level 10

| Step | Quick slot (first ready skill wins) |

| 2 | Whirlwind Slice |
| 3 | Heart Gore → Ambush, Savage Roar |
| 4 | Whirlwind Slice |

## Gear

Equip quest upgrades as you get them. While leveling, aim for +5 on your weapon and guard before spending on other gear [@gamingtools-build-assassin-leveling-2026-10].

## Where to level

Poeta (Elyos) or Ishalgen (Asmodians) until level 10 [@fextralife-regions-2026-10].
