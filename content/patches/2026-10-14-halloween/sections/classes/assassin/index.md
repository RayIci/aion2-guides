## The Assassin in one minute

- The melee **damage** class: daggers, damage in short burst windows, and it wants to hit bosses **from behind** [@corpus-assassin-guide-2026-10].
- The burst comes from **Illusive Clone**, which removes Heart Gore's cooldown; pair it with Swift Contract for 20% Combat Speed [@corpus-assassin-guide-2026-10; @corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10].
- Creators rate its damage ceiling among the highest; the cost is positioning, low survivability and a high-ping penalty [@corpus-assassin-guide-2026-10].
- Creators most often name Assassin, Gladiator and Templar for 1v1 and small fights [@corpus-best-pvp-class-2026-10].
