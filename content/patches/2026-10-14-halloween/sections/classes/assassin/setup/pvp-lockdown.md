**Who it's for:** Fextralife's PvP build: a skirmish and duel setup built on hidden openers, a guaranteed Airborne and escapes [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].

## Skills and milestones

- **Shadow Walk** to 15: lets you re-enter Stealth in combat [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].
- **Aerial Bind** to 5 (range 4 → 20 m) and 10 (Aerial Slaughter chain) [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].
- **Shadow Fall** to 16: also works on Blinded targets [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].
- **Evasion Contract** to 10 (+30% Move Speed) and **Illusive Clone** to 10 (+20% Critical Damage Boost) [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].
- Passives: Heightened Sixth Sense, Revitalization Contract, Apply Poison [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].

## The macro

Add only the blue keys, in order, delay 10 ms. Keep control, openers and defensive skills out of it so you can time them: creators agree on keeping defensive and mobility skills manual [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10; @fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].

## How to play

::::play
1. **Open from Stealth:** Shadow Walk (1), come in from the side or back, Shadowstrike (2) for the Stun, then Shadow Fall (3) for the Knockdown [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].
2. **If the Stun fails:** Blind them with Flash Slice or Infiltrate (Q), then Shadow Fall (needs its level-16 Specialization) [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].
3. **Guaranteed Airborne:** build 5 Insignias with Savage Roar and Ambush (8) from behind, then Aerial Bind (6), then Insignia Explosion for the kill [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].
4. **Burst:** Illusive Clone (7) only once the control has landed [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].
5. **Defend:** Evasion Contract (E) against Slows and Roots; Defiance (4) only for hard control; when they use Defiance, back off until their immunity ends [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].
::::
