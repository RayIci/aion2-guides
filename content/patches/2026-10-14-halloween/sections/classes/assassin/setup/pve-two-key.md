:::quick{title="Quick steps"}
1. Build the two stacked slots below: the **macro slot** (key 5) and the **buff button** (key 1) [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
2. Bind the macro key under Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro, then build the macro from the Skill screen (K) with the Macro button; add key 5 as its only entry, 10 ms [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10; @corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].
3. Hold right click (the macro) and left click (Quick Slice) together; press key 1 a few times to open each burst window [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
4. Skills and Stigmas: the same as every PvE setup ([Level 45: set up](/classes/assassin/setup#pve-skills-to-raise)) [!opinion].
:::

**Who it's for:** you want the macro for your damage but to choose the moment of every burst, with one button for all your buffs. This is aion2-meta's in-game macro setup [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].

The Assassin's damage comes from Heart Gore, Insignia Explosion and the basic attack. Heart Gore only triggers on a Critical Hit, so in the stack it fires whenever it can [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].

Throw Shadowblade and Shadowstep are both Stigmas; aion2-meta's data comes from Korea's six slots, so with Global's four you may have only one of them [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10; !opinion].

## The setup

::::play
- **Macro key (one slot, from the bottom):** Insignia Explosion, Ambush, Heart Gore, Savage Roar. That slot is the macro's only entry, on right click; hold it with left click (Quick Slice), which restores MP and, with its Specialization, takes 1 s off Insignia Explosion's cooldown per hit [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
- **Buff button (one slot, from the bottom):** Swift Contract, then Illusive Clone: under Swift Contract, Illusive Clone's animation is shorter [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
- **By hand:** Triniel's Dagger, Storm Rampage and Shadowstrike, plus Throw Shadowblade and Shadowstep to move [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
::::

## Things to know

- Each skill preset has its own macro, and only one runs at a time; switch presets with **Alt + Num4, Num5, Num6** [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
- A slot holds up to four skills; the bottom one fires first [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10; @corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].
- **Skill Queue** is on by default; leave it on. If your basic attack stops coming out while you hold the macro and your MP drops, turn it off [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
- Use only the game's own macro: aion2-meta warns that NCSOFT bans accounts for mouse and third-party macros [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
