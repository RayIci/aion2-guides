:::quick{title="How to fill your boards"}
1. Your Daevanion boards are **shared by every skill preset**, so you pick one plan for the character, not one per setup [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].
2. Open the boards as they unlock: **Nezekan** (level 12), **Zikel** (20), **Vaizel** (30), **Triniel** (40). Each of these four adds one level to every active skill [@corpus-gladiator-daevanion-2026-10; @aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
3. **Plan A** gives the exact nodes for the 136 points you have at 45; **Plan B** numbers every node in the order to open it, so you can follow it point by point [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10; @aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
4. **For PvP:** once the first four are where you want them, put further points into **Azphel** (opens at 45; PvP stats) in Plan B's order [@corpus-gladiator-daevanion-2026-10; @aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
5. Hover a node to see what it gives, or open the list under each board (on a phone, use the list) [!opinion].
:::

You get 4 Daevanion points a level from level 12: 136 by level 45 [@gamingtools-character-levels-2026-10]. The board costs are 1 point for a small stat, 2 for a passive, 3 for a skill +1 and 4 for a big stat [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].

## What creators say

Each of the first four boards (Nezekan, Zikel, Vaizel, Triniel) adds one level to every Assassin active skill, which is how Heart Gore, Quick Slice and Insignia Explosion get past 10 [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10].

- gaming.tools opens Nezekan (46 points), Zikel (32), Vaizel (4) and Triniel (3) at 45 and plans the rest for later [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].
- **Azphel** (opens at 45) has only PvP stats: fill the first four boards first [@corpus-gladiator-daevanion-2026-10].
