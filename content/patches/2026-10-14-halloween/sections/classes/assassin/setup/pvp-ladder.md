**Who it's for:** What top Korean PvP players run: Illusive Clone, Swift Contract, Triniel's Dagger and Throw Shadowblade (96–99%), the same three damage skills as PvE, and more points in Shadowstrike, Whirlwind Slice and Defiance [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].

## Skills and milestones

- Push **Heart Gore**, **Quick Slice** and **Insignia Explosion** to 20 [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
- Raise **Shadowstrike** (28% at 20+ in PvP vs 2% in PvE), **Whirlwind Slice** (26% vs 2%) and **Defiance** (41% vs 1%) [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10].
- Korea has six Stigma slots; the extras are Evasion Stance (95%), Shadow Walk (94%) and Savage Fang (90%). Swap one in for Throw Shadowblade when you need it [@aion2meta-assassin-2026-10; !opinion].

## The macro

Add only the blue keys, in order, delay 10 ms. Keep control, openers and defensive skills out of it so you can time them: creators agree on keeping defensive and mobility skills manual [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10; @fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].

## How to play

::::play
1. Slow them with Throw Shadowblade (E), then Shadowstrike (Q) behind them for the Stun [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-throw-shadowblade-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10; !opinion].
2. Burst by hand: Illusive Clone (1), Swift Contract (2), Triniel's Dagger (3) [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10; !opinion].
3. Hold the macro: key 5 for Insignia Explosion and Heart Gore, key 6 for Whirlwind Slice and Shadow Fall on the stunned target [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10; !opinion].
4. Defiance (4) only on the control that leads into a kill [@fextralife-assassin-builds-2026-10].
::::
