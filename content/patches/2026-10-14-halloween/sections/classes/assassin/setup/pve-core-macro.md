:::quick{title="Quick steps"}
1. Put your skills on the keys as in the bar below [!opinion].
2. Create a macro with **key 5 only**: that row is your core combo [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10; @corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].
3. Hold right mouse (the macro) and left mouse (Quick Slice); press everything amber yourself [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10].
4. Skills and Stigmas: the same as every PvE setup ([Level 45: set up](/classes/assassin/setup#pve-skills-to-raise)) [!opinion].
:::

**Who it's for:** You want the macro to do only your core damage, and to time your burst (Illusive Clone, Swift Contract, Triniel's Dagger) yourself. [!opinion]

Grobs's macro is one row: Insignia Explosion, Heart Gore, Savage Roar while levelling, with Savage Fang added in front from level 45 [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10]. He puts Shadowstrike and Infiltrate on the same key and uses whichever is off cooldown [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10]. This setup takes Grobs's fourth Stigma, **Savage Fang**; with Evasion Stance or Throw Shadowblade instead, drop Savage Fang from the row and put the other Stigma on a free key [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10; !opinion]. Creators keep self-buffs, Stigma burst, dashes and charge skills out of the macro [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].

## The macro

::::play
1. Skill menu → **Macro** → **Add macro** [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].
2. Add **key 5** as the only step; delay 10 ms [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10; @gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].
3. Hold it with left mouse (Quick Slice), so the game weaves in animation cancels [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10].
::::

## How to play

::::play
1. Illusive Clone (1) and Swift Contract (2) [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10].
2. Shadowstrike (Q) behind the boss for its Critical Damage Boost [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10].
3. Triniel's Dagger (3) right after both: at level 10 it cuts the cooldowns already running [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10].
4. Hold right mouse (the macro) and left mouse (Quick Slice) together; reposition with Q or Flash Slice (E) to stay behind [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10].
5. Storm Rampage (6) only on a staggered target, when Illusive Clone and Swift Contract are on cooldown [@corpus-assassin-pve-build-2026-10].
::::
