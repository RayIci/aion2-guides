The Assassin hides in stealth before striking from behind. It fights with a dagger in each hand, attacks fast, and builds combos around poison and stun skills [@gamingtools-assassin-2026-10]. The game tags it as **DPS**; it is open to both factions [@gamingtools-assassin-2026-10].

## What it plays like

- **Insignias.** Savage Roar engraves an Insignia on each hit (Savage Fang engraves 5), and Insignia Explosion cashes them in for more damage and Stun chance [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10].
- **Crits.** Heart Gore triggers on a Critical Hit and restores 100 MP; Illusive Clone removes its cooldown for 10s [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-illusive-clone-2026-10].
- **From behind.** Ambush and Triniel's Dagger deal 30% more damage from behind; Flash Slice and Shadowstrike move you behind the target [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10].
- **Stealth.** Shadowstep (3s) and Shadow Walk (20s, out of combat) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10].
- **No class resource:** it uses MP; Quick Slice restores 100 MP per hit [@gamingtools-assassin-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10].
