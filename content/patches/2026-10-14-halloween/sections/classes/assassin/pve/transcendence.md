Transcendence is weekly, seasonal content for 5 players, with stages in each dungeon; each dungeon and stage has seasonal traits, and the setup changes every season [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

| Stage | Item level to enter |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1,600 |
| 2 | 1,900 |
| 3 | 2,200 |
| 4 | 2,500 |

[@corpus-item-level-2026-10; @raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10]

Stage 3 is the first stage that drops Unique Arcana cards [@raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10]. Arcana cards add 20 (Common), 40 (Rare), 60 (Epic) or 80 (Unique) item level each, across five slots [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].

Use the **Group DPS** preset [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].
