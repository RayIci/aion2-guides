Three level-45 presets from gaming.tools' Assassin endgame build, one per job: **Solo Farm**, **Solo Single Target** and **Group DPS** [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]. All three share the same Daevanion boards; each preset has the same full skill and Stigma point totals, including the Ascension quest's Stigma Shard [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].

- **Solo Farm:** Use Savage Roar and Flash Slice across packs, then take area Whirlwind Slice openings. Keep your defensive stance ready while you build and spend Insignias. [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]
- **Solo Single Target:** Build Insignias on one enemy and use your clone during a clear opening. Recover health through Shadow Fall and Storm Rampage when their conditions are met. [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]
- **Group DPS:** Stay behind the boss to build Insignias and spend them on heavy hits. Use your clone during clear attack windows and keep your defensive stance ready for incoming damage. [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]

## Stigmas

The same four in every preset: **Illusive Clone** (level 10), **Swift Contract** (level 5), **Triniel's Dagger** (level 5), **Evasion Stance** (level 5). A Stigma only counts once it sits on a quick slot [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].

## Daevanion

The shared boards open Nezekan (46 points), Zikel (32), Vaizel (4) and Triniel (3) now, and plan the rest for later points [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].

This is one site's recommendation. No second source has been checked yet, so treat the exact levels as a starting point [!opinion].
