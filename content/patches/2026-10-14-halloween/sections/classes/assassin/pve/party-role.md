The Assassin is tagged DPS [@gamingtools-assassin-2026-10]. In a party, gaming.tools' **Group DPS** preset says: stay behind the boss to build Insignias and spend them on heavy hits. Use your clone during clear attack windows and keep your defensive stance ready for incoming damage. [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]

- Ambush and Triniel's Dagger hit 30% harder from behind, so stand behind the boss while the tank holds it [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10].
- Triniel's Dagger also raises all of the target's skill cooldowns by 5% of their duration [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10].

Which party compositions work best is not written yet [!unverified].
