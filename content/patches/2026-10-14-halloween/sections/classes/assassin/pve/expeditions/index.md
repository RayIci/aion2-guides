Party dungeons for 1–5 players (Urugugu Canyon and Vakron Sky Island need 2–5), each with an Exploration and a Conquest difficulty. Exploration can be done solo; Conquest unlocks at level 45 if your item level is high enough [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10; @gamingtools-exp-krao-cave-exploration-2026-10; @gamingtools-exp-urugugu-canyon-exploration-2026-10].

Rewards come from a reward cube that costs Odyle Energy to open (20–40 per run), with one claim per week; party dungeons reset on Wednesday at 16:00 server time [@gamingtools-exp-krao-cave-exploration-2026-10; @gamingtools-exp-krao-cave-conquest-2026-10; @gamingtools-systems-weekly-resets-2026-10].
