The Assassin fights with a held generator (left mouse) plus a macro that presses a fixed list of quick slots. Each quick slot casts the first ready skill in its list, so the lists below are priorities, not a strict order [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].

::::play
## Core loop

- **Build and spend Insignias.** Savage Roar, Ambush (from behind) and Heart Gore engrave Insignias; Insignia Explosion spends them for more damage and Stun chance, and the build keeps 2 stacks after each Explosion [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10].
- **Crits feed Heart Gore.** It triggers on a Critical Hit and restores 100 MP; Illusive Clone removes its cooldown for 10s, but Heart Gore still needs a critical hit to go off while the clone is up [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-illusive-clone-2026-10; @gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].
- **Stun opens Shadow Fall.** Shadowstrike (Stun 3s) and Insignia Explosion stun; Shadow Fall needs a stunned target and knocks it down [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10].
- **Burst window:** Swift Contract (+20% Combat Speed for 10s), Illusive Clone and Triniel's Dagger (+30% from behind) share one quick slot [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-swift-contract-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10].
- **Evasion Stance (key 1) right before a big hit**; it evades all damage briefly and restores 110 MP and Stamina [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10].
- The macro's Ambush is automatic, which trades control over positioning and animation timing for fewer inputs [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].

### Solo Farm

Use Savage Roar and Flash Slice across packs, then take area Whirlwind Slice openings. Keep your defensive stance ready while you build and spend Insignias. [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]

1. Shadowstrike → Ambush
2. Whirlwind Slice → Flash Slice, Shadow Fall
3. Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore, Storm Rampage, Savage Roar
4. Whirlwind Slice → Flash Slice, Shadow Fall
5. Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore, Storm Rampage, Savage Roar
6. Swift Contract → Illusive Clone, Triniel's Dagger
7. Infiltrate (key E)
### Solo Single Target

Build Insignias on one enemy and use your clone during a clear opening. Recover health through Shadow Fall and Storm Rampage when their conditions are met. [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]

1. Shadowstrike → Ambush, Flash Slice
2. Shadow Fall → Whirlwind Slice
3. Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore, Storm Rampage, Savage Roar
4. Swift Contract → Illusive Clone, Triniel's Dagger
5. Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore, Storm Rampage, Savage Roar
6. Shadow Fall → Whirlwind Slice
7. Infiltrate (key E)
### Group DPS

Stay behind the boss to build Insignias and spend them on heavy hits. Use your clone during clear attack windows and keep your defensive stance ready for incoming damage. [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]

1. Swift Contract → Illusive Clone, Triniel's Dagger
2. Swift Contract → Illusive Clone, Triniel's Dagger
3. Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore, Storm Rampage, Savage Roar
4. Shadowstrike → Ambush, Flash Slice
5. Whirlwind Slice → Shadow Fall
6. Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore, Storm Rampage, Savage Roar
7. Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore, Storm Rampage, Savage Roar
8. Infiltrate (key E)
