- The navigator lists the daily dungeon **Unknown Fissure** at level 30: stage-based, up to 14 attempts a week [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].
- The client's daily-dungeon list has one entry, **Daeva Bio-Research Base**, recommended level 30. Its completion rewards are Enhance Stones scaled by points: 10,000 stones at 10,000 points, 9,500 at 9,500, and so on down [@gamingtools-daily-bio-research-base-2026-10].

These may be the same content under two names; not confirmed [!unverified].
