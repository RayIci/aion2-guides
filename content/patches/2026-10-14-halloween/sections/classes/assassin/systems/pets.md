Pets come from Soul Crystals dropped by monsters. They are mainly used as mounts, have extra effects depending on their type, and you can raise your Insight about each pet you own [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

- Pets unlock at character level 10 [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].
- Pet types (Genus): **Cogni**, **Fera**, **Natura**, **Varian** and **Special** [@gamingtools-pet-collection-2026-10].
- Owning more pets gives collection rewards: 100 Soul Crystals at 5, 10, 20, 30 and 40 pets owned, and 10 Empathy Candy at 50 and 100 [@gamingtools-pet-collection-2026-10].
- Empathy Candy lets you transform into your pet for a few minutes [@fextralife-pet-guide-2026-10].

Which pets help an Assassin is not written yet [!unverified].
