Substance Morph converts gathered materials, crystal-type items and enhancement materials into other items: excess crystals and fragments become complete items or crafting resources (from level 10) [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

- Converting Sealed Wings into Enhance Stones is capped at 20 per server per week [@nc-global-notes-2026-10-07].
- During the server transfer period (from the 14 Oct 2026 patch), “Instant Clear Ticket: Daily Dungeon (Bound)” and “Soul Crystal (Bound)” move from Shop → Wind Breeze Merchants → Special to Substance Morph → Special → Wind Breeze Merchants [@nc-global-notes-2026-10-14].
