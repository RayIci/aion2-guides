**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 832 + 155% Attack damage to a target within 4m. The caster moves back and gains Stealth for 3s. [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 45s |
| Cost | 100 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- 40% chance to rotate and inflict Stun for 3s on hit (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10]
- Engraves 5 Insignias for 10s on hit (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10]
- +200 Evasion for 10s on use (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10]
- -15s cooldown (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build; PvP picks are on each PvP mode's build page.
