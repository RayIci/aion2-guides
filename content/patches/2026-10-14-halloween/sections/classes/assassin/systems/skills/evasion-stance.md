**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Grants guaranteed evasion of all damage for a brief duration. Restores 110 MP and Stamina once on Evasion. [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Cooldown | 20s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- +1 consecutive use (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10]
- Changes to mobile skill (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10]
- +20% Move Speed for 3s on Evasion (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10]
- Restore 10% HP once after successfully evading (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10].

In gaming.tools' PvE build: Group DPS (Stigma, level 5), Solo Farm (Stigma, level 5), Solo Single Target (Stigma, level 5) [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]. PvP picks are on each PvP mode's build page.
