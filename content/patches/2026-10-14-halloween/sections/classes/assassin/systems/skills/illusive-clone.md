**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-illusive-clone-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Removes **Heart Gore** cooldown and deals 20% of damage dealt as extra damage for 10s. [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-illusive-clone-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Cooldown | 90s |
| Cost | 200 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- +10% Status Effect Chance for the duration (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-illusive-clone-2026-10]
- +20% Critical Damage Boost for the duration (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-illusive-clone-2026-10]
- x1.5 **Exploit Weakness** effect for the duration (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-illusive-clone-2026-10]
- 50% chance to deal extra damage on Critical Hit for the duration (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-illusive-clone-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-illusive-clone-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-illusive-clone-2026-10].

In gaming.tools' PvE build: Group DPS (Stigma, level 10), Solo Farm (Stigma, level 10), Solo Single Target (Stigma, level 10) [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10]. PvP picks are on each PvP mode's build page.
