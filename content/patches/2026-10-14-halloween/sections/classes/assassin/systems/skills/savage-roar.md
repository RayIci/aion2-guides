**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 68 + 83.5% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 4m and engraves 1 Insignia for 10s. 2 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 5.9 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cost | 120 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |
| Chain role | Opening Skill |
| Chains into | Savage Back Kick |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10].

- -20% MP consumed (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10]
- +20% Skill Speed (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10]
- Deals up to 12% more damage when less targets are hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10]
- -1s **Shadowstrike** cooldown on hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10]
- Pulls nearby targets on using **Savage Smash** (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-roar-2026-10]
