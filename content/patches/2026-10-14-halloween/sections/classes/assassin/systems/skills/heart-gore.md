**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Triggers on landing a Critical Hit, dealing 111 + 122% Attack damage to up to 4 enemies within 4m and restores 100 MP. 5 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies, Rectangle 5.9 m, up to 4 targets |
| Cooldown | 5s |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10].

- Absorbs 0.7% HP (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10]
- Engraves 1 Insignia for 10s on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10]
- Up to +20% Critical Hit when less targets hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10]
- Multi-Hit on hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10]
- Resets **Heart Gore** cooldown on landing a Critical Hit (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-heart-gore-2026-10]
