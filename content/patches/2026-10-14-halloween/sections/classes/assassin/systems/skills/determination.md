**Passive skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-determination-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 228 + 37.5% Attack damage when landing an attack on a target with 30% HP or less. Cooldown: 1s [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-determination-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Max skill level | 40 |
