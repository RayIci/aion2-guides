**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Rushes to a target within 20m and deals 1074 + 200% Attack damage, removing 1 buff. Deals 386 + 72% Attack extra damage based on the number of buffs removed. 20 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 20 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 90s |
| Cost | 200 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- Removes up to 2 buffs (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10]
- Rotates and inflicts Stun for 3s when removing 2 or more buffs (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10]
- Removes up to 3 buffs (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10]
- -30s cooldown (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build; PvP picks are on each PvP mode's build page.
