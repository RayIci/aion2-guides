## How skills grow

- **Skill Points:** levels 2–10 of each active and passive skill cost 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 4, 4 and 4 SP (21 SP per skill), each step three character levels apart [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10].
- **Max skill level:** 40 for active and passive skills and 40 for Stigma skills, per the data pages [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10].
- **Specializations:** slots at skill level 8, 12 and 20 on active skills [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-quick-slice-2026-10].
