**Stigma skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Deals 837 + 156% Attack damage to a target within 4m, with a 50% chance to inflict Airborne for 3s. Has a 100% chance to inflict Airborne for 3s on targets with 5 stacks of Insignia. 100% chance to inflict Airborne on NPC targets. 20 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 4 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 120s |
| Cost | 150 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

- +16m **Aerial Bind** range (skill level 5) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10]
- Adds **Aerial Slaughter** Chain Skill (skill level 10) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10]
- x1.5 **Ambush Stance** effect for 10s on first strike of **Aerial Bind** (skill level 15) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10]
- -30s cooldown (skill level 20) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10]

### Learning

Learned through Stigma at character level 22, after the quest “Assassin” [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10].

Levels 1–20 all need character level 22. Cost per level, in the data page’s Stigma units: 5 levels at 1 stigma, 5 levels at 2 stigma, 5 levels at 4 stigma, 5 levels at 8 stigma [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10].

Not in gaming.tools' PvE build; PvP picks are on each PvP mode's build page.
