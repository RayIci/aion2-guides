**Passive skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-revitalization-contract-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Increases the caster's Status Effect Resist by 17%. Increases Status Effect Resist by 10% for 5s when hit while afflicted with Stun, Knockdown, Airborne, Frost, or Fear (up to 10 stacks). Immediately restores 35% Max HP when HP is 10% or less. Healing Cooldown: 60s Status Effect Resist Cooldown: 1s [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-revitalization-contract-2026-10]

|---|---|

| Max skill level | 40 |
