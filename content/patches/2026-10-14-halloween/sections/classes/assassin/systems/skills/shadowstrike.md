**Active skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Moves behind a target within 20m and deals 52 + 100% Attack damage, rotates, and inflicts Stun for 3s. 10 Stagger Gauge Damage [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Range | 20 m |
| Target | Enemies |
| Cooldown | 20s |
| Cost | 150 MP |
| Max skill level | 40 |

### Specializations

Specialization slots open at skill level 8, 12, 20 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10].

- +1s Stun duration (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10]
- +20% caster Critical Damage Boost for 10s on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10]
- 50% chance to inflict Seal for 5s on hit (skill level 8) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10]
- 50% chance to inflict Root for 5s on hit (skill level 12) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10]
- Changes to AoE Skill (skill level 16) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10]
