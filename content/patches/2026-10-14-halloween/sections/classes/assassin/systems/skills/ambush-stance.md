**Passive skill** for the Dagger [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-stance-2026-10].

At skill level 1: Grants Ambush Stance for 10s when using skills that rush or involve movement. Ambush Stance: Has a 50% chance to deal 152 + 39% Attack damage on landing an attack. Cooldown: 1s [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-stance-2026-10]

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | Self |
| Max skill level | 40 |
