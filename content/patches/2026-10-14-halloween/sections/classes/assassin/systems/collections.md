Account and character stat sources outside your gear:

- **Titles** come from achievements; you can equip up to 3 at once for bonus stats (from level 7) [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].
- **Closet** stores outfits. Extracting gear from the Cube turns its skin into an outfit (from level 10) [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10]. Collecting skins gives Skin Collection Bonuses [@gamingtools-guild-collections-2026-10].
- **Pantheon**: place colossi, statues and artwork you find around Atreia; placed decorations give effects, listed under Main Menu → Stats (from level 22) [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].
- **Empyrean Lord's Monolith**: use Empyrean Traces found around the world to raise a Monolith's level for rewards (from level 16) [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

Statues for the Pantheon also come from the Ascension quests: Dream Fragment, Artifact of Eternity and Asahr's Mirror [@gamingtools-ascension-rewards-2026-10].
