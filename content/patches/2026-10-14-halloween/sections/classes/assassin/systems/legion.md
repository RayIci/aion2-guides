Legions are AION 2's guild system. Creating or joining one unlocks a Legion hall and other benefits (from level 16) [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

Legion levels give every member bonuses [@gamingtools-guild-collections-2026-10]:

| Legion level | Bonuses |
|---|---|
| 1 | EXP gain +1% |
| 5 | EXP gain +2%, HP 16, MP 16, Evasion 1, Accuracy 1, Attack 1 |
| 10 | EXP gain +3%, HP 36, MP 36, Evasion 2, Accuracy 2, Attack 2, Defense 2 |
| 20 | EXP gain +3%, HP 80, MP 70, Evasion 5, Accuracy 5, Attack 4, Defense 4 |

A Legion holds up to 128 members [@gamingtools-guild-collections-2026-10].
