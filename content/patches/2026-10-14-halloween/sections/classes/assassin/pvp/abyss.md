:::quick{title="Before you queue"}
1. **Gear:** your PvE gear, with the **Fierce Battle Amulet** instead of the Revelation Amulet (+20% PvP Damage Boost) [@gamingtools-item-database-2026-10].
2. **Setup:** start with [PvP 1 (Stealth lockdown)](/classes/assassin/setup/pvp-lockdown): Shadow Walk lets you pick your fights; also try [PvP 2 (Ladder)](/classes/assassin/setup/pvp-ladder) (in organised groups) [!opinion].
3. **Press by hand:** the amber keys on your setup's skill bar: control, openers and defensive skills [@corpus-macros-guide-2026-10; !opinion].
4. **For this mode:** Swap Evasion Stance for **Shadow Walk** (20 s Stealth out of combat) to pick your fights; 94% of Korean PvP players carry it [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10; @aion2meta-assassin-2026-10; !opinion].
:::

## The mode

The Abyss (Reshanta) is open faction-vs-faction PvP from level 45 (item level 1,000): Chaotic Lower, Middle and Upper Reshanta [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10; @gamingtools-abyss-2026-10; @corpus-item-level-2026-10]. Stored entry time is capped at 7 hours [@nc-global-notes-2026-10-14], and Abyss points reset Wednesday 16:00 [@gamingtools-systems-weekly-resets-2026-10].

## Combo and defence

::::play
### Kill combo

1. **Shadowstrike**: moves behind a target within 20 m and stuns it for 3s [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10].
2. **Shadow Fall**: only on a stunned target, knocks it down for 3s [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10].
3. **Triniel's Dagger** and **Ambush** from behind, each +30% [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-ambush-2026-10].
4. **Insignia Explosion**: spends the Insignias from Savage Roar, Ambush and Heart Gore; more stacks mean more damage and a higher Stun chance [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10; @gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].

Burst variant with the 1v1 Stigmas: **Savage Fang** (5 Insignias) → **Aerial Bind** (always Airborne at 5 Insignias) → Insignia Explosion at full stacks [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10; !opinion].

### Defence

- Break Stun, Knockdown, Airborne, Grab, Frost or Fear with **Defiance**, which then makes you immune for 5s [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10].
- Root and Slow: **Evasion Contract** removes both and makes you immune; Seal needs a Healing Potion [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-contract-2026-10; @gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10].
- **Evasion Stance** right before a big hit: it evades all damage for a moment [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10; @gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].
- **Shadowstep** to drop out of a fight into 3s of Stealth [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10].
::::

Defiance removes Stun, Knockdown, Airborne, Grab, Frost and Fear, then grants 5 s of immunity, on a 60 s cooldown [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10].

## Tactics

- Hunt players away from their group: approach in Shadow Walk, which ends as combat starts, and open with Shadowstrike [!opinion].
- Close the gap with Shadowstrike (20 m), Spiral Slice (20 m) or Infiltrate (10 m, behind the target) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10].
- Your best hits want the target's back: Shadowstrike, Infiltrate and Flash Slice all move you behind or past it [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10].
- Watch for Seal: keep a Healing Potion ready, since neither Defiance nor Evasion Contract breaks it [@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10].
