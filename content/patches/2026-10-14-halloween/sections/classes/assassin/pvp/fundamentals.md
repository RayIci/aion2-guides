## Crowd control and how to break it

| Effect | Type | What it does | Removed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stun | Impact | immobilized and incapacitated | Defiance or cleansing |
| Knockdown | Impact | immobilized and incapacitated | Defiance or cleansing |
| Airborne | Impact | immobilized and incapacitated | Defiance or cleansing |
| Grab | Impact | immobilized and incapacitated | Defiance or cleansing |
| Fear | Mental | forced to move away from the caster | Defiance or cleansing |
| Taunt | Mental | forced to move to and attack the caster | Defiance or cleansing |
| Frost | Ailment | immobilized and incapacitated; broken by Frost Burst | Defiance or cleansing |
| Sleep | Mental | immobilized and incapacitated; may break when hit | cleansing |
| Polymorph | Mental | immobilized and incapacitated; may break when hit | cleansing |
| Paralyze | Ailment | immobilized and incapacitated | cleansing |
| Seal | Mental | cannot use skills | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Root | Ailment | immobilized | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Blind | Ailment | more attacks fail | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Lethargy | Ailment | lower Combat Speed | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Slow | Ailment | lower Move Speed | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Poison | Ailment | damage over time, less potion recovery and healing | Healing Potions or cleansing |
| Bleed | Ailment | damage over time | Healing Potions or cleansing |

[@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10]

**Defiance** removes Stun, Knockdown, Airborne, Grab, Frost and Fear and then grants Tenacity (status-effect immunity) for 5s, on a 60s cooldown [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10]. The crowd-control table also lists Taunt as removable by Defiance [@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10]. Sleep, Polymorph, Paralyze, Seal and Root need cleansing or potions instead [@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10]. Spend Defiance on the control that leads into a kill, not the first one you take [!opinion].

## Assassin tools for PvP

- **Evasion Stance** (Stigma): evades all damage for a moment and restores 110 MP and Stamina, 20s cooldown [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10].
- **Evasion Contract** (Stigma): removes Slow and Root, grants immunity to both and +200 Evasion for 10s, 90s cooldown. It covers the Root that Defiance can't [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-contract-2026-10; @gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10].
- **Shadowstep** (Stigma): a hit, then you step back into Stealth for 3s, 45s cooldown [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstep-2026-10].
- **Shadow Walk** (Stigma): Stealth for 20s, out of combat only; entering combat removes it [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-walk-2026-10].
- **Smoke Bomb** (Stigma): 10 m hit with Blind for 5s; Blind makes more attacks fail [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-smoke-bomb-2026-10; @gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10].
- **Spiral Slice** (Stigma): 20 m rush that removes 1 buff and hits harder for each buff removed [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10].
- **Triniel's Dagger** (Stigma): +30% from behind and raises all of the target's skill cooldowns by 5% of their duration [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10].
- **Aerial Bind** (Stigma): 50% Airborne for 3s, always on a target with 5 Insignias; **Savage Fang** engraves those 5 [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-savage-fang-2026-10].

## Your control

Shadowstrike always stuns for 3s and puts you behind the target; Shadow Fall only works on a stunned target and knocks it down for 3s [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10]. Insignia Explosion's Stun chance grows with the Insignias on the target [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-insignia-explosion-2026-10]. Flash Slice and Infiltrate can Blind (40% and 50%) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10].

## PvP stats

Willpower raises Status Effect Resist and Dexterity raises Critical Hit Resist; Zikel's board adds Perfect Resist [@gamingtools-systems-stat-conversion-2026-10]. Revitalization Contract adds 17% Status Effect Resist, and Rear Smite 1.5% PvP Damage Boost [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-revitalization-contract-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-rear-smite-2026-10].
