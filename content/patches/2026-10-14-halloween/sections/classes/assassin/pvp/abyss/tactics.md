The Abyss (Reshanta) is open faction-vs-faction PvP from level 45 (item level 1,000): Chaotic Lower, Middle and Upper Reshanta [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10; @gamingtools-abyss-2026-10; @corpus-item-level-2026-10]. Stored entry time is capped at 7 hours [@nc-global-notes-2026-10-14], and Abyss points reset Wednesday 16:00 [@gamingtools-systems-weekly-resets-2026-10].

- Hunt players away from their group: approach in Shadow Walk, which ends as combat starts, and open with Shadowstrike [!opinion].
- Close the gap with Shadowstrike (20 m), Spiral Slice (20 m) or Infiltrate (10 m, behind the target) [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-spiral-slice-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10].
- Your best hits want the target's back: Shadowstrike, Infiltrate and Flash Slice all move you behind or past it [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-infiltrate-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-flash-slice-2026-10].
- Watch for Seal: keep a Healing Potion ready, since neither Defiance nor Evasion Contract breaks it [@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10].

More detailed tactics need vetted PvP sources [!unverified].
