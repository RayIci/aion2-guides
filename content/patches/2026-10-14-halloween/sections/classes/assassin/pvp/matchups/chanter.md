**Chanter**: Healer, DPS, uses a Staff [@gamingtools-classes-2026-10; @nc-global-skins-2026-10].

Kill it first, from behind. **Triniel's Dagger** raises all its skill cooldowns, heals included, by 5% of their duration, and **Apply Poison** can cut its Incoming Heal by 12% [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-triniels-dagger-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-apply-poison-2026-10]. Start with Shadowstrike so the Stun covers your burst [!opinion].

This class's own skills and crowd control are not researched yet, so there are no specific counters here [!unverified].
