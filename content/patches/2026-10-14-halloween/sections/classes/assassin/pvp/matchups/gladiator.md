**Gladiator**: Tank, DPS, uses a Greatsword [@gamingtools-classes-2026-10; @nc-global-skins-2026-10].

A damage race: break its first control with Defiance only if it leads into a kill, keep Evasion Stance for its biggest hit, and land Shadowstrike → Shadow Fall from behind first [!opinion].

## Its kit

The Gladiator's main threat is Mocking Blade, which always knocks you down for 3s, followed by Overhead Slam, which only hits a knocked-down target [@gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10]. It also has Defiance, Focused Block (a guaranteed Parry) and Stigmas such as Forced Restraint, a Seal that Defiance can't remove [@gamingtools-skill-defiance-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-focused-block-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-forced-restraint-2026-10; @gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10].

- Keep Defiance for Mocking Blade's knockdown, so Overhead Slam has nothing to hit [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-mocking-blade-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-overhead-slam-2026-10; !opinion].
- Don't open into Focused Block: bait it with Quick Slice, then start your chain [@gamingtools-skill-focused-block-2026-10; !opinion].
- Forced Restraint's Seal needs a Healing Potion [@gamingtools-systems-crowd-control-2026-10].
