**Assassin**: DPS, uses a Dagger [@gamingtools-classes-2026-10; @nc-global-skins-2026-10].

A damage race: break its first control with Defiance only if it leads into a kill, keep Evasion Stance for its biggest hit, and land Shadowstrike → Shadow Fall from behind first [!opinion].

## The mirror

The other Assassin has the same tools: Shadowstrike's guaranteed Stun into Shadow Fall, Defiance, Evasion Stance, and Stigmas such as Aerial Bind and Smoke Bomb [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadowstrike-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-shadow-fall-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10; @gamingtools-skill-assassin-smoke-bomb-2026-10].

- Its Defiance has a 60s cooldown: bait it with Shadowstrike, then chain Shadow Fall after it is spent [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-defiance-2026-10; !opinion].
- Don't swing into Evasion Stance: it evades everything for a moment, so wait it out [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-evasion-stance-2026-10; !opinion].
- Watch its Insignia count: at 5 stacks Aerial Bind always launches you [@gamingtools-skill-assassin-aerial-bind-2026-10].
