No vetted PvP macro exists yet. Start from the **Group DPS** macro on PvE → PvE macros and put your PvP Stigmas on its slots [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10; !opinion].

Keep Defiance, Evasion Stance, Evasion Contract and Shadowstep out of the macro and press them by hand, since manual input always takes priority and timing them is the whole point [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10; !opinion]. The PvE macro's automatic Ambush gives up control over positioning, which matters more in PvP [@gamingtools-build-assassin-pve-2026-10].
