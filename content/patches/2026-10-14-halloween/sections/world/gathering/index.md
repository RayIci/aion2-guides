Gathering is done with **Essence Extraction**, a minigame with a Success Gauge and a Failure Gauge; you learn gathering skills that change the gauges [@gamingtools-systems-gathering-2026-10]. It unlocks at level 10 [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10].

## Gathering skills

- **Proficient Handling:** raises Success Gauge gain, +5 per level up to +50 at level 10 [@gamingtools-systems-gathering-2026-10].
- **Delicate Touch:** lowers Failure Gauge gain, −8 per level up to −80 [@gamingtools-systems-gathering-2026-10].
- **Lady Luck:** a chance to multiply the Success Gauge gain, 8% at level 1 up to 40% (×2.5 or ×4) at level 10 [@gamingtools-systems-gathering-2026-10].

Levels 1–5 are Novice and 6–10 Professional; each level costs gathering points (1 to 5) [@gamingtools-systems-gathering-2026-10].

## Node types

| Type | Used for | Nodes |

| Aquatic Life | cooking ingredients | 5 |
| Fruit | cooking ingredients | 2 |

| Herb | herbs for Alchemy (potions) | 4 |
| Log | logs for Handicrafting | 2 |
| Odyle | Odyle for crafting | 6 |
| Ore | Orichalcum Ore for ingots (Blacksmithing, Armorsmithing) | 2 |
| Vegetable | cooking ingredients | 4 |

Every gathering result can also drop a **Poko's Skin Bundle (Bound)**, and herbs and logs drop **Life Crystal** and **Seed of Detection** as bonuses [@gamingtools-gather-1500000-2026-10].
