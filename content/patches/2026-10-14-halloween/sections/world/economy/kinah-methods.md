Ranked by what the sources can back up; the order itself is the author's reasoning [!opinion].

1. **Expedition cubes.** Every Krao Cave Exploration cube holds 25,000 Kina (Bound) [@gamingtools-exp-krao-cave-exploration-2026-10]. For the same 40 Odyle Energy, Urugugu Canyon and Vakron Sky Island pay 300,000 Kina, 50% more than Draupnir and Krao Cave's 200,000 [@raidrelay-gear-score-2026-10]. Spend Odyle on the highest tier you can enter.
2. **Crafting and selling.** Some recipes sell for more than their materials cost; see Crafting → Profit checks for dated numbers [@gamingtools-crafting-calculator-2026-10].
3. **Gathering and selling materials.** Gathered materials sell on the market, but since 14 Oct 2026 listing them is limited by your gameplay history [@nc-global-notes-2026-10-14]. Selling at all needs membership (market access) [@gamingtools-membership-2026-10].
