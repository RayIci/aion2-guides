## Membership

**Special Quai Membership** (account-wide, 30 days, extendable to 60) [@gamingtools-membership-2026-10]:

- Access to the Market and the Quna Exchange
- Access to the Wind Breeze Merchants and Remote Storage
- Fast queue
- More Odyle Energy Cube selections in Expeditions, Transcendence and Sanctuary
- Max Odyle Energy storage raised to 840
- Max Shugo Festival Reward Keys raised to 21

## Daeva Pass

The Season 1 passes, Dawn Legion (Elyos) and Ishalgen Mercenary (Asmodians), run 30 Sep to 16 Dec 2026, with a Basic and a Premium track of 101 levels each. A shorter pass, Planet's Call, runs 30 Sep to 28 Oct 2026 with 51 levels [@gamingtools-daeva-pass-2026-10]. The Battle Pass is one of the game's paid options [@nc-global-launch-faq-2026-09].

Whether the membership is worth it depends on whether you trade: without it you can't use the Market [@gamingtools-membership-2026-10; !opinion].
