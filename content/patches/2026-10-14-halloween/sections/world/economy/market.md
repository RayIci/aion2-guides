- The **Market** and the **Quna Exchange** need the Special Quai Membership (30 days, extendable to 60) [@gamingtools-membership-2026-10]. The membership is the paid access to the player-driven economy [@nc-global-launch-faq-2026-09].
- Sales are taxed: the crafting calculator shows sales "after tax and fee" [@gamingtools-crafting-calculator-2026-10]. The exact rate is not listed [!unverified].
- Since 14 Oct 2026, how many gathering materials you can list depends on your gameplay history [@nc-global-notes-2026-10-14].
- Prices differ per server; gaming.tools' crafting calculator reads them per market server [@gamingtools-crafting-calculator-2026-10].
