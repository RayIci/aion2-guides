:::quick{title="What do you want to do?"}
1. **Farm a material:** open [Materials](/world/materials). Every material has its best spot per faction, a map with the spot circled and a numbered loop.
2. **Craft gear:** [Gear crafting paths](/world/crafting/gear-paths) lists every tier's inputs, each linked to where it comes from.
3. **Level a profession:** pick it under [Crafting](/world/crafting), then check [Profit checks](/world/crafting/profit) before buying inputs.
4. **Make Kina:** [Making Kina](/world/economy/kinah-methods).
5. **Find what is on a map:** [Maps](/world/maps) shows each zone with its areas, gathering nodes, farm targets and field bosses.
:::
