Crafting unlocks at level 10 [@gamingtools-navigator-2026-10]. There are five professions, plus **Transfer Crafting**, used for the higher crafted gear [@gamingtools-crafting-calculator-2026-10]; creators describe upgrading crafted gear in place [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].

| Profession | Makes |
|---|---|
| Blacksmithing | Dagger, Greatsword, Guard, Longsword, Mace, Materials (Blacksmithing) |
| Armorsmithing | Cloak, Gloves, Helm, Legs, Materials (Armorsmithing), Pauldrons, Shoes, Top |
| Handicrafting | Bow, Bracelet, Earrings, Materials (Handicrafting), Necklace, Ring, Staff |
| Alchemy | Manastone, Materials (Alchemy), Orb, Potion, Scroll, Spellbook |
| Cooking | Food |

[@gamingtools-crafting-calculator-2026-10]

## Crafting mastery

Each profession levels from 1 to 100: Novice for 1–50 and Professional for 51–100, with 1 skill point per level [@gamingtools-systems-crafting-2026-10].

## The crafted gear line

Weapons, armor and accessories share one line of names, e.g. for greatswords: Orichalcum → Expert's Orichalcum → Artisan's Orichalcum → True Dragon Lord (bows and staves use Yggdrasil instead), plus Transfer Crafting recipes for the Splendent True, White, Splendent White, Wise and Splendent Wise Dragon Lord versions [@gamingtools-crafting-calculator-2026-10]. The first crafted endgame tier is True Dragon Lord (Elyos) or Star Dragon Lord (Asmodians) at item level 62, upgraded in place up to 102 [@corpus-item-level-2026-10].
