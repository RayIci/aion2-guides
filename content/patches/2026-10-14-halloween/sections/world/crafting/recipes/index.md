What each recipe needs, one page per profession: **Blacksmithing**, **Armorsmithing**, **Handicrafting**, **Alchemy**, **Cooking**.

The calculator prices each ingredient at its cheapest source, bought or crafted, and weighs the result by success and combo chances; a failed attempt uses up its ingredients [@gamingtools-crafting-calculator-2026-10]. Figures are for **10 attempts** at Siel (Europe, Elyos) market listings on 17 Oct 2026; listings are not sale prices, and buying many units can cost more [@gamingtools-crafting-calculator-2026-10]. So the shopping list can hold more than 10 of an input, and it buys a crafted input outright when that is cheaper than making it [!opinion]. Prices on your server will differ [!opinion].

For which recipes make money right now, see **Profit checks**.
