:::quick
1. Pick your piece below and note its tier: each tier from Expert's up takes the **Splendent** version of the same piece from the tier before [@gamingtools-item-database-2026-10].
2. Farm the inputs. Every material name links to its page with the best spot, a map and a route.
3. Craft the tier-1 piece until you get a Splendent result, then move up a tier [!opinion].
4. True Dragon Lord pieces then upgrade in place through Transfer Crafting (Splendent True, White, Wise Dragon Lord) [@gamingtools-crafting-calculator-2026-10].
:::

The crafting calculator says a successful attempt can trigger a combo whose result replaces the normal one [@gamingtools-crafting-calculator-2026-10]; the Splendent pieces the recipes ask for are presumably that combo result [!unverified].
